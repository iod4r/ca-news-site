    Kazakhmys invests 2.7 billion tenge in safety at Zhomart mine

    Kazakhmys has invested 2.7 billion tenge in industrial safety measures at its Zhomart mine. The investment focuses on enhancing underground air quality control, equipping machinery with methane sensors, and modernizing ventilation systems. These upgrades aim to improve safety for the approximately 1,200 workers at the mine, which extracts about 3.8 million tons of ore annually.

    Kazakhstan Investigates Gasoline Quality Amidst Consumer Complaints About AI-95 and AI-98

    Kazakhstan's Ministry of Trade is investigating the quality of AI-95 and AI-98 gasoline following consumer complaints. The probe will examine the entire fuel supply chain, from the Pavlodar Petrochemical Plant (PNHZ) to gas stations.
