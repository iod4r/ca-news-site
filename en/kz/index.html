    Kazakhstan Allows State to Buy Shares of Systemically Important Banks Facing Collapse

    Starting September 6, the government or a national holding company can acquire shares of systemically important banks at risk of collapse using state funds. The National Bank and the Agency for Regulation and Development of the Financial Market have approved the procedure for such intervention.

    Kazakhstan Tightens Energy Audit Requirements for Businesses

    Starting September 6, Kazakh businesses must more accurately calculate energy consumption per unit of production. Audits will include measurements in winter and summer, and assessments of modernization benefits. "Eyeballing" savings is prohibited; calculations must be supported by measurements and technical data.

    A deputy head of the Bayzak district police department in the Jambyl region, Lieutenant Colonel Talgat Sarsenov, has been found guilty of accepting a bribe.
