Over 300 Illegal Migrants Identified in Kyrgyzstan's Issyk-Kul Region

Authorities in Kyrgyzstan's Issyk-Kul region identified 303 foreign nationals staying illegally. During raids on workplaces employing foreign nationals, documents of over 1,000 people were checked. Fines totaling 2.7 million soms were imposed for migration law violations, and deportation proceedings are underway for eight individuals.
