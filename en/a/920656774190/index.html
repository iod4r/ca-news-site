Tokayev's Great-Nephew Owns Universities Receiving State Funds, Investigation Reveals

An investigation by Azattyq and OCCRP found that Nurassyl Medeu, President Tokayev's great-nephew, holds 95% stakes in two universities located in state buildings and receiving state contracts. One university was established on Tokayev's initiative. Following the investigation's publication, Medeu announced the transfer of a sports university to the state, calling it a 'gratuitous gift to his native country'.
