Uzbekistan Launches NarxStat Platform for Monitoring Prices of Goods and Housing

Uzbekistan's National Statistics Committee has launched NarxStat, an open platform providing data on consumer prices, inflation, and the cost of various goods across the country. The service offers weekly average prices for food and fuel, housing market indices, and producer price indices, with data available for download in CSV and JSON formats.
