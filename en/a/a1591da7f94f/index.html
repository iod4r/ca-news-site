Nurlan Zhakypov to Head Water Sports Federation Pending Investigation Outcome

Nurlan Zhakypov, Chairman of the Samruk-Kazyna Fund, will continue as President of the Kazakhstan Water Sports Federation until an ongoing investigation concludes. The Minister of Tourism and Sports stated that Zhakypov's detention has not impacted the federation's work. No personnel decisions regarding the organization's leadership will be made until the investigation is complete. Zhakypov has led the federation since March 2025.
