Uzbekistan to Partially Cover Diesel Fuel Costs for Agricultural Enterprises

Uzbekistan will temporarily subsidize a portion of diesel fuel costs for agricultural enterprises purchasing fuel through commodity exchanges. This measure applies to fuel bought between July 1 and December 1, 2026, if the price exceeds 15 million soms per ton. The subsidy is capped at 2 million soms per ton.
