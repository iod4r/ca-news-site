Turkmenistan Hosts International Investment Forum with Global Participation

Turkmenistan held an international investment forum in Ashgabat, attracting participants from Central Asia, the USA, UK, Japan, Singapore, Saudi Arabia, Germany, China, and France. The event focused on analyzing international standards for reducing methane emissions and improving the legal framework for the energy sector, aligning with national development priorities and the country's 'open door' policy.
