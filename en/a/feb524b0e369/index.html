Air Arabia Selected to Transport Kyrgyz Pilgrims to Hajj in 2027

Air Arabia has been chosen to transport 6,060 Kyrgyz pilgrims to Saudi Arabia for Hajj in 2027. The decision followed a competition by an interdepartmental commission. In previous years, Kyrgyz pilgrims were transported by Saudi Arabian airlines Flyadeal and Jazeera Airways.
