Uzbek President Aims for 1 Million Tons of Cotton in Bukhara Within Three Years

Uzbekistan plans to reclaim 100,000 hectares of desert land in the Bukhara region for cotton and wheat cultivation and orchard development. President Mirziyoyev announced a goal to produce 1 million tons of cotton in Bukhara within three years, significantly increasing the current target. He also noted that Uzbekistan has harvested nearly 5 million tons of cotton this year.
