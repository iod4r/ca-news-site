Study: 81% of Almaty Residents Consider Air Pollution Dangerous, Few Take Action

A study by Almaty Air Initiative found that 81% of Almaty residents view air pollution as a health risk, with a quarter considering relocation. Despite high emotional concern (70.3/100), knowledge (37.6/100), willingness (37.6/100), and actual actions (16.8/100) are significantly lower, indicating a gap between concern and behavior.
