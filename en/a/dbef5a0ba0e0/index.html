COP31 Climate Summit in Turkey Concludes Without Agreement

The COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, concluded without adopting a final declaration. Despite two days of negotiations, participating countries failed to agree on commitments to phase out fossil fuels and provide compensation to developing nations. Key disagreements centered on the lack of a direct call to abandon oil, gas, and coal in the draft "Antalya Agreements."
