Uzbekistan to Introduce Dual Education System in Universities from 2027/2028 Academic Year

Uzbekistan will implement a dual education system in universities starting from the 2027/2028 academic year. This system will train personnel based on orders from employers, with a commitment to hire graduates. The program is established by a presidential decree aimed at improving dual education.
