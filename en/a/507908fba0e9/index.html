ICC Halts Investigation into Alleged Crimes by US Troops in Afghanistan

The International Criminal Court (ICC) has halted its investigation into alleged crimes by US troops in Afghanistan, citing "significant limitations" in carrying out investigations and obtaining evidence. The ruling also ends the probe into alleged crimes by the Islamic State group's affiliate in Khorasan Province. The prosecutor's office has appealed the decision.
