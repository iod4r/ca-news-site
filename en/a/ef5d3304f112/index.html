Kazakhstan Detains Heads of Wealth Fund and Railway Company on Corruption Charges

Kazakhstan's National Security Committee has detained the heads of the national wealth fund Samruk-Kazyna and the state railway company KTZ on suspicion of corruption. The detentions raise concerns about corporate governance in key economic sectors and could delay the planned IPO of Kazakh Railways. The KNB has not identified other individuals taken into custody.
