Central Asian nations to establish electricity trading platform

Tajikistan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, and Uzbekistan plan to create a common platform for electricity trading. This initiative aims to allow countries to sell surplus power and purchase electricity to cover deficits. The project, supported by the World Bank, is expected to launch a pilot market within 18 months.
