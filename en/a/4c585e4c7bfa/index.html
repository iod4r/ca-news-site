ADB Approves $60 Million Loan for Livestock Development in Five Uzbekistan Regions

The Asian Development Bank (ADB) has approved a $60 million loan to support livestock development in Uzbekistan, benefiting approximately 29,000 small farms across five regions. The project aims to help farms cope with drought, heat, and water scarcity by improving pasture management, fodder cultivation, and water access. Digital technologies and veterinary services will also be modernized.
