Tajikistan to expand electricity supplies to Afghanistan's Badakhshan province

Pamir Energy and Afghanistan's Da Afghanistan Breshna Sherkat (DABS) have amended an electricity supply agreement. Supplies will continue to border areas of Badakhshan province, serving 6,900 consumers. The agreement also includes expanding electricity exports via Pamir Energy's networks and constructing new power lines.
