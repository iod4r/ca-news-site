Kazakhstan Considers Electricity Tariff Adjustments for 2027

Kazakhstan may see another round of electricity tariff adjustments in 2027, according to an energy sector expert. Following a recent increase of 10% to over 100% for energy-producing organizations, further changes will depend on the upcoming heating season's performance and subsequent repair costs. The expert believes tariff hikes are not a primary driver of inflation.
