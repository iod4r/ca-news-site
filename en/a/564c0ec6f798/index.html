Kazakhstan's Average Salary Jumps 12-Fold Over Two Decades Amidst Economic Growth

The average salary in Kazakhstan has increased nearly 12 times, from 40,800 tenge in 2006 to 486,400 tenge in Q2 2026. Average monthly expenses per person rose approximately 9.5 times during the same period. Nominal GDP grew significantly, contributing to increased household incomes and expenditures, though unevenly across different goods.
