Russian Doctors Arrive in Uzbekistan for 'Mission of Kindness' Program

Seven specialists from RZD-Medicine have arrived in Uzbekistan for the international volunteer mission 'Mission of Kindness,' running from October 26-31. The Russian doctors are holding professional meetings with Uzbek colleagues, sharing expertise, and providing consultations. The delegation includes specialists in cardiology, oncology, urology, gynecology, ophthalmology, and traumatology, visiting medical centers in Tashkent and Samarkand.
