Iran Reports Renewed Increase in Afghanistan Drug Cultivation Despite Taliban Ban

Iran's Anti-Narcotics Headquarters reports a renewed increase in drug cultivation in Afghanistan, despite the Taliban's ban. Secretary-General Hussein Zolfaghari stated this trend was confirmed by a recent UNODC report, which previously indicated significant rises in opium poppy cultivation and production. Zolfaghari described drug trafficking as a global challenge exceeding the threat of wars.
