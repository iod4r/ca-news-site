Kazakhstan Vice President Chairs Citizenship Commission Meeting

Vice President Yerlan Karin chaired a meeting of the Commission on Civil Affairs, recommending the acceptance of 255 individuals into Kazakhstan's citizenship. The commission also recommended denying citizenship to 43 applicants. The Vice President noted a declining trend in renouncing citizenship, indicating high social optimism.
