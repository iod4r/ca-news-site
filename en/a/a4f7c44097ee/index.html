Kazakhstan: State prosecution seeks 12 years for activist Sanjar Bokayev

In Almaty, the state prosecution has requested a 12-year prison sentence for civil activist and blogger Sanjar Bokayev. The trial, taking place in the Almalinsky District Court, also involves charges against two other individuals. Bokayev and others face multiple charges including fraud and spreading false information.
