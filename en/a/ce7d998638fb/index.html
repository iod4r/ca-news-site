Kazakhstan Plans Three Nuclear Power Plants, Secures Uranium Reserves for 100 Years

Kazakhstan plans to build three nuclear power plants to meet growing electricity demands and establish a nuclear industry cluster. The country, second globally in uranium reserves and a major producer, views uranium as a strategic resource for energy and economic development. Plans also include training specialists and developing nuclear technologies for medicine and agriculture.
