Aktobe authorities revoke permit for protest on disability rights

Aktobe city authorities have revoked a permit for a protest concerning the social rights of citizens with disabilities. The permit, initially granted for December 3rd, was cancelled due to ongoing emergency repair work by 'Aqtobe Su-Energy Group' at the designated protest site. The authorities stated that holding the peaceful assembly at the specified location and time was not possible.
