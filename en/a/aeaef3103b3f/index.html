Uzbekistan to Implement Mandatory Psychological Diagnostics for Educators and Students

Uzbekistan will introduce mandatory psychological diagnostics for educators and students at least once per academic year. This initiative, starting in the 2026/2027 academic year for preschools and schools, and in 2027/2028 for vocational and higher education, aims to identify and support individuals needing psychological assistance. Results will be analyzed by AI to categorize individuals into 'psychologically stable,' 'needing support,' or 'requiring active support.'
