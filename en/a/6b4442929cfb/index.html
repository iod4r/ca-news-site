KazMunayGas Plans to Spend 4.2 Billion Tenge on Five-Year Vehicle Lease

KazMunayGas is seeking to lease 34 vehicles, including four-wheel-drive pickups, for five years at a cost of approximately 4.2 billion tenge (excluding VAT). The tender, divided into three lots for different fields, aims to secure vehicles manufactured no earlier than five years prior to the service year. The contract is set to run from January 2027 to December 2031.
