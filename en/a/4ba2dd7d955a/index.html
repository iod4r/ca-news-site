Kyrgyzstan President Denies Ordering Language Test for Candidates Amidst Political Dispute

Kyrgyzstan's presidential press secretary denied President Sadyr Japarov ordered a language test for all presidential candidates. This follows politician Omurbek Suvanaliev's claim that the president instructed his press secretary to ensure candidates pass the 'Kyrgyztest'. Suvanaliev stated he and Siyymyk Japikeev met with the president, who allegedly mandated the test. The press secretary confirmed Japikeev passed the test independently.
