Magnitude 3.7 Earthquake Recorded in Southern Kyrgyzstan on November 8

An earthquake with a magnitude of 3.7 occurred in southern Kyrgyzstan on the morning of November 8, 2026, at 05:14. The tremors were felt with an intensity of 3 points in the village of Bor-Döbö, Osh region. The epicenter was located 5 km southeast of Bor-Döbö at a depth of 15 km. This region has experienced previous seismic activity.
