Taliban Authorities Force Afghan Men to Restrict Female Family Members

Afghan men and women have reported that Taliban authorities are compelling men to impose strict restrictions on female family members. These restrictions reportedly include forbidding women from leaving their homes and withholding their cell phones. The Taliban's actions aim to enforce their interpretation of social and personal freedoms for women.
