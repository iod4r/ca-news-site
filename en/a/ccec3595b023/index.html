Taliban Fighters Kill Former Security Officer in Takhar Province

Taliban fighters reportedly shot and killed Ahmad Farid Kohistani, a former security officer, in front of his family in the Warsaj district of Takhar province. Kohistani had served as a police officer under the previous government. The motive for the killing, which occurred on September 27, remains unclear. This incident follows reports of killings and mistreatment of former security forces since the Taliban's return to power.
