Kazakhstan Inflation Forecast Lowered to 9.5% for 2026

Economists participating in the National Bank's macroeconomic survey now expect Kazakhstan's inflation to reach 9.5% by the end of 2026, a decrease from the 10% forecast in August. Projections for 2027 and 2028 were also revised downwards. These figures represent the expected year-on-year increase in consumer prices.
