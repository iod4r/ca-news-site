Kazakhstan Ready to Supply Food to Gulf Countries, President Tokayev States

President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev announced Kazakhstan's readiness to become a reliable partner for Gulf countries in ensuring food security. Speaking at a Kazakh-Omani business forum in Astana, he highlighted opportunities for agricultural cooperation and proposed Oman as a logistics partner. Tokayev also suggested exploring joint ventures in energy and mining, areas where both nations have existing experience.
