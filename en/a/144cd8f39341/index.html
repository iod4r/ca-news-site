Underwater Internet Cable Between Kazakhstan and Azerbaijan to Launch in October

The Trans-Caspian fiber-optic communication line between Kazakhstan and Azerbaijan is scheduled to launch in October. Approximately 390 kilometers of cable have been laid from Sumgayit, Azerbaijan, to Aktau, Kazakhstan. This new route will connect the digital infrastructure of both countries, creating a high-speed data corridor between Asia and Europe.
