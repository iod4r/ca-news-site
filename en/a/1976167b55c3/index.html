Nearly 70 Percent of Kazakh Citizens Trust the Country's Judicial System

Kazakh President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev met with Supreme Court Chairman Aslambek Mergaliyev. The President was briefed on the judicial system's performance over nine months, digitalization efforts, and measures to enhance public trust. Trust in the system rose from 55.2% in 2023 to 69.7% currently, according to the National Statistics Bureau.
