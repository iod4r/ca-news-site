Uzbekistan Studies South Korean Digital Social Protection Experience

A delegation from Uzbekistan's National Social Protection Agency completed a two-week training course in South Korea. They studied the Korean pension system and the digitalization of social protection services. The delegation visited the 'Government 24' portal and learned about the Social Security Information Service (SSiS), which uses AI to support social workers and has reduced application processing times.
