Kyrgyzstan MP Claims Unequal Conditions for Students Preparing for ORT Exam

A Member of Parliament in Kyrgyzstan has stated that preparation for the General Republican Test (ORT) creates unequal conditions for students, as paid tutoring and services are not accessible to all families. MP Alisher Kozuev argued that students from wealthier families have a greater chance of achieving high scores, while the majority are overlooked. He proposed integrating ORT preparation into the school curriculum.
