UNICEF Warns Yemen's Taiz Water Supplies at Risk for 1.6 Million Children

UNICEF warns that escalating conflict and fuel shortages in Yemen's Taiz governorate threaten water supply systems for three million people, including 1.6 million children. Disruptions have tripled water delivery costs, and diesel shortages risk shutting down water pumps. Children in Taiz face essential survival services being lost.
