US Congressman Expresses Concern Over Kazakhstan's Delayed Accreditation for RFE/RL Journalists

US Congressman Robert Aderholt voiced concern over Kazakhstan's government delaying accreditation for Radio Free Europe/Radio Liberty (RFE/RL) journalists. He highlighted RFE/RL's long history of providing fair and impartial information to the Kazakh people and its over 30 years of operation within Kazakhstan. Aderholt, a co-author of a bill granting trade privileges to Kazakhstan, stressed the importance of RFE/RL's continued free operation.
