Only Three Kazakhstan Regions Ready to Renovate Dilapidated Housing by 2029, Says Deputy

Only three regions in Kazakhstan are prepared to address the issue of dilapidated housing by 2029, according to Kurultai deputy Amirbek Ospanbekov. Out of 61,367 apartment buildings, 1,037 are in a dilapidated state. Over the past three years, only 352 such buildings were demolished and 200 new ones constructed, representing 0.3% of the total housing stock.
