Kyrgyzstan's state budget revenue projected to reach 1.15 trillion soms by 2031

Kyrgyzstan's state budget revenues are projected to reach 791.1 billion soms in 2027 and 1,148.6 trillion soms by 2031, according to the Ministry of Finance. The ministry forecasts budget revenues to be 28.9 percent of GDP in 2027, declining to 23.1 percent by 2031. Expenditures are expected to rise from 758.7 billion soms in 2027 to 1,049.4 trillion soms in 2031. The ministry plans to maintain a budget surplus, with funds allocated for economic development and public debt repayment.
