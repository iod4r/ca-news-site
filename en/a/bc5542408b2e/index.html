Kazakhstan to Expand Internet Access in Rural Areas by 2027

Kazakhstan plans to significantly expand internet access in rural areas by 2027, aiming to connect 92% of villages with fiber optic internet. Difficult-to-reach areas will receive satellite internet. The project involves upgrading 3G base stations to 4G and expanding 5G coverage.
