Aiba Amur Becomes Ninth Candidate for Kyrgyzstan Presidential Election

Aiba Amur submitted his application to the Central Election Commission on November 11, becoming the ninth contender for the presidential election scheduled for January 27, 2027. He is self-nominated. Amur is reportedly linked to companies involved in oil product manufacturing and tourism services. Candidates must gather 30,000 signatures, prove state language proficiency, and pay a 1 million som deposit.
