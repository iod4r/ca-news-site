Tajikistan Pledges Doubled Emissions Cuts with Donor Support

Tajikistan has updated its climate commitments, pledging to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 10% by 2030 and 15% by 2035, with potential increases to 25% and 40% if sufficient funding and technology are received. These updated Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) were announced at the launch of the regional 'Sustainable Climate Landscapes of Central Asia' (CLiCA) program.
