Astana Special Public Service Center Employees Involved in Illegal Driver's License Scheme

An investigation in Astana has concluded into the illegal issuance of 74 driver's licenses through Special Public Service Center (SpSC) employees and intermediaries. The scheme involved intermediaries providing answers during exams and SpSC staff falsifying records of practical test completion. The licenses have been invalidated, and the case is heading to court.
