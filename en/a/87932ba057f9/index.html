New $171.4 Million Substation Operational for Uninterrupted Tashkent Power Supply

A new 500-kilovolt, 1602-megavolt-ampere substation named 'Khalka' has been commissioned in Uzbekistan's energy system. This facility will enhance the stability of electricity supply to Tashkent and parts of the Tashkent region, optimizing load distribution. The project, costing $171.4 million, also includes 285 km of new power lines and aims to reduce emissions by decreasing reliance on fuel oil.
