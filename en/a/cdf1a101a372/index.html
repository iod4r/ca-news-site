Tajikistan launches 'Bus' operation to inspect passenger transport

From September 14 to 23, 2026, Tajikistan is conducting a nationwide 'Bus' operation to inspect passenger transport. Organized by the State Automobile Inspectorate, the operation aims to prevent road accidents, improve service quality, and ensure compliance with traffic rules. Inspectors will focus on technical condition and service standards of buses and minibuses.
