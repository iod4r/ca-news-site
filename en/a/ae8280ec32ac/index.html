Kazakhstan to Allocate 6.5 Trillion Tenge from National Fund for Projects

Kazakhstan will allocate approximately 6.5 trillion tenge from the National Fund for key social and infrastructure projects in 2027, declared as the Year of Nationwide Large-Scale Construction. The plan includes urban environment renewal, digital city twins, housing, and utility modernization, as announced by Deputy Prime Minister Serik Zhumangarin.
