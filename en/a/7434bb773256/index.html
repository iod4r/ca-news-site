Kazakhstan to Increase Oil Supplies via Azerbaijan to 1.7 Million Tons

Kazakhstan intends to increase oil supplies via Azerbaijan by over 40% next year, reaching 1.7 million tons. KazMunayGas is negotiating increased volumes for the Baku-Tbilisi-Ceyhan pipeline. The route's viability depends on economic conditions and tariffs, but it will not replace the Caspian Pipeline Consortium (CPC).
