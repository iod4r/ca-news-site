Turkish police apprehend 35 Afghan migrants attempting illegal entry into Greece

Turkish police arrested 35 Afghan nationals, including 19 children, in Balıkesir Province while they were attempting to cross into Greece illegally. Four suspected organizers of migrant smuggling were also detained. This follows Pakistan's arrest of 82 Afghan refugees. Poverty and unemployment in Afghanistan are driving migration.
