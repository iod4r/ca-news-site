Tajik Migrants Warned of Stricter Deportation Rules in Russia from November 2026

From November 2026, Russia will implement stricter deportation rules for foreign citizens, expanding the list of administrative offenses leading to expulsion. The number of articles in the Code of Administrative Offenses allowing deportation will increase from 22 to 45. This affects Tajik migrants, who may face deportation for offenses beyond immigration violations, including public order and social media posts.
