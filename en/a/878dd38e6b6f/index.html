Uzbekistan and Poland Strengthen Parliamentary and Economic Ties

Uzbekistan and Poland are enhancing their inter-parliamentary and economic cooperation. A Polish delegation visited the Legislative Chamber of the Oliy Majlis to discuss strengthening parliamentary diplomacy and expanding exchanges. Both sides emphasized activating friendship groups and exchanging experiences in lawmaking and local governance. Economic indicators show steady growth, with trade turnover reaching $397.6 million in 2025.
