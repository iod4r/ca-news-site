Climate Change to Disproportionately Affect Vulnerable Groups in Kyrgyzstan

Climate change in Kyrgyzstan is predicted to most severely impact vulnerable populations, including women, children, the elderly, people with disabilities, and low-income families. The National Adaptation Plan highlights that over 57% of the population relies on agriculture, making them susceptible to droughts and water shortages. Women, often responsible for household water and food, face increased burdens as resources decline.
