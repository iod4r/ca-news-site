Kazakhstan to enable remote medical image analysis by doctors nationwide

Kazakhstan is introducing a system to unify X-ray, CT, and MRI results, allowing doctors in different clinics to remotely study medical images. This initiative, part of healthcare law amendments, aims to improve the quality and accessibility of medical care by facilitating information exchange between regional and national medical centers.
