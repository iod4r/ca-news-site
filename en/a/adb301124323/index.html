Political blogger Syrgak Kenzhebaev detained abroad and brought to Kyrgyzstan

Kyrgyzstan's Ministry of Internal Affairs announced the detention of Syrgak Kenzhebaev, the creator of the YouTube channel 'Er-Syrgak'. Kenzhebaev had been critical of the authorities for the past two months. He was detained abroad and subsequently transported to Kyrgyzstan. The ministry did not provide further details on the circumstances of his detention.
