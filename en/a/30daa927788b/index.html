Industrial Park for Non-Ferrous Metals to Be Established in Northern Tajikistan

An industrial park for non-ferrous metals will be established in northern Tajikistan through cooperation between Tajikistan's Ministry of Industry, China's Non-Ferrous Metals Industry Association, and Tibet Everest Company. The collaboration aims to develop deep processing of mineral raw materials, increase added value, localize production, create jobs, and expand export capacity.
