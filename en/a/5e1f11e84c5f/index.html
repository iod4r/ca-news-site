11,000 foreign nationals left Kyrgyzstan under amnesty in 2026, says Deputy Foreign Minister

Approximately 11,000 foreign nationals illegally staying in Kyrgyzstan departed the country under an amnesty in 2026, according to Deputy Foreign Minister Seitek Zhumakadyr uluu. He stated that around 500-600 illegal migrants may remain, and deportation efforts are ongoing. Funds from additional fees collected from foreigners are used for these measures.
