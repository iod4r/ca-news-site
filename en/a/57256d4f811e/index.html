Kazakh President Tokayev Dismisses Defense Ministry Officials After Fatal Incident

Kazakh President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev has dismissed several officials from the Ministry of Defense following a fatal incident at sea. The dismissals include the first deputy minister and chief of the General Staff. The actions follow an incident where 17 servicemen were swept out to sea, resulting in 14 deaths. Two criminal cases have been initiated.
