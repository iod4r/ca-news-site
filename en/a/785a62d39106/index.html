Navoiy Region to Launch Glass Product Manufacturing Project with Chinese Company

A $76 million investment project for a glass container manufacturing plant is set to be implemented in Navoiy Region, Uzbekistan. The project, in collaboration with China's Qingdao Rongtai Glass Products, will utilize quartz raw materials. The plant aims for full production capacity within five years, with the first phase launching within a year.
