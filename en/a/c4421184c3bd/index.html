Kazakhstan to Phase Out 3G by 2027, Expand Mobile Internet to 92% of Villages

Kazakhstan plans to completely phase out 3G technology by the end of 2027, while expanding 4G and 5G network coverage. The country aims to provide mobile internet access to 92% of its villages by then. Investments in the telecom sector are expected to exceed 1.3 trillion tenge over three years.
