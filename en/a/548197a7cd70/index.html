Almaty Physics and Mathematics School Leadership Replaced After Student Poisoning Incident

The leadership of the Republican Physics and Mathematics School in Almaty has been changed following a mass poisoning of students. Almaz Kungozhin, a PhD in mathematics and a former student of the school, has been appointed as the new director. The previous director, Meirbek Meirhan Edigeruly, resigned after 47 students were hospitalized with severe intestinal infections, a number that later rose to 58. Investigations revealed violations in food storage and daily sampling of prepared meals in the school's dining facility.
