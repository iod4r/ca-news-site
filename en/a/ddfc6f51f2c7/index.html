Four Men Convicted in Kazakhstan for Stealing Electronics from China-Europe Trains

Four men have been convicted in Kazakhstan for stealing electronics from cargo trains traveling from China to Europe. The total damages amounted to nearly 633 million tenge. The thefts, which included laptops and game consoles, occurred on trains destined for Poland. Some stolen goods were later sold in Almaty. The convicted men received sentences of up to six years and seven months.
