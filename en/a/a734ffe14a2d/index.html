Kazakhstan Court Overturns Early Release of Former Akim Karim Kokirekbaev

The Almaty Regional Court overturned a decision to release former Akim of Zhambyl region, Karim Kokirekbaev, early due to health reasons. The appellate court found gross violations of legal requirements by the first instance court. Kokirekbaev's condition does not meet the criteria for release.
