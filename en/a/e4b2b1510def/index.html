Afghan UN Envoy Urges International Action on Human Rights, Women's Rights

Afghanistan's UN Representative Nasir Ahmad Faiq called for urgent international action to improve human rights in Afghanistan, particularly for women and girls. He stressed restoring rights to education and work, protecting vulnerable groups, and supporting migrants. Faiq also emphasized accountability for violations and meaningful participation of Afghans in decisions.
