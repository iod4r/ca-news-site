Proposal to Erect Monument to Jusup Abdrakhmanov in Kazakhstan

A scientific conference in Almaty proposed erecting a monument to Jusup Abdrakhmanov, former chairman of the Council of People's Commissars of the Kirghiz ASSR, in either Almaty or Astana. Kazakhstani scientists suggested this initiative to honor his role in saving Kazakh refugees and to commemorate Kyrgyz-Kazakh brotherhood.
