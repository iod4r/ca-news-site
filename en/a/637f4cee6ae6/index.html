Astana Airport Expansion to Acquire Land from Air Astana, SCAT, and Kazцинк

Astana's city administration plans to expropriate over 1,000 hectares across 57 land plots to expand the capital's airport. The affected lands include those belonging to airlines Air Astana and SCAT, mining company Kazцинк, and the Prosecutor General's Office. Expropriation will occur between February 2027 and February 2028.
