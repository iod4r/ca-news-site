Kyzylorda Region Hospitals Overpaid Private Clinics 73 Million Tenge, Prosecutors Find

Hospitals in Kazakhstan's Kyzylorda region overpaid private clinics by 73 million tenge for medical services, according to the regional prosecutor's office. State medical organizations paid contractors beyond contractual amounts in 2023 and 2024. The prosecutor's office intervened, disciplinary action was taken against responsible officials, and the overpaid funds were recovered through court decisions.
