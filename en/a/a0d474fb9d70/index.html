48.5 kg of Narcotics Seized at Uzbek Border Crossing from Afghanistan

Uzbekistan's State Security Service (SSS) reported the seizure of 48.5 kg of narcotics at the "Ayritom" border customs post. The drugs, identified as 39 kg of hashish and 9.5 kg of opium, were found hidden in a truck originating from Afghanistan. A criminal case has been initiated.
