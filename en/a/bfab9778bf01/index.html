Taliban search home of former Afghan police chief in Uruzgan, seize weapons

Taliban fighters raided the home of General Matiullah Khan, former police chief of Uruzgan province, on Saturday evening. Sources reported disrespectful treatment of his family and a search of the property. The Taliban's police command announced the seizure of four Kalashnikov rifles and 250 rounds of ammunition from the former official's residence.
