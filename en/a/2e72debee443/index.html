Kazakhstan Employers Forced to Post Fake Job Vacancies, Says Deputy

Kazakh employers are compelled to post fictitious job vacancies, according to Kurultai deputy Arman Kozhahmetov. He stated that the HR Enbek platform imposes excessive, non-legislative requirements, forcing businesses to list vacancies even when a candidate is already chosen. This practice misleads job seekers and creates an artificial labor market.
