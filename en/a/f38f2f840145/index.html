UN Experts Demand Pakistan Investigate Civilian Killings in PoJK

UN human rights experts have formally requested Pakistan to investigate civilian deaths in Pakistan-occupied Jammu and Kashmir (PoJK). At least 96 protesters were reportedly killed since June 2026, with many victims aged 15-35. Experts emphasize that peaceful protest should not be equated with terrorism and firearms should not be used to disperse assemblies.
