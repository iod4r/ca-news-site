Oil Shipments Through Strait of Hormuz Decline Amid Increased IRGC Attacks on Vessels

Oil transit through the Strait of Hormuz nearly halved in the second week of November compared to the first, according to preliminary data. This follows a period of significant recovery in shipments, which had even surpassed pre-war levels in October. The decrease comes after a rise in attacks by the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) on vessels.
