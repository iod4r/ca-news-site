EBRD Approves €15 Million Solar Power Plant Project in Kyrgyzstan

The European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD) has approved a €15 million project to build a 20 MW solar power plant in Kyrgyzstan's Chuy region. The 'Chakan Solar Power Plant' will be constructed for state-owned OJSC 'Chakan GES' and includes a 6 MWh battery storage system.
