Uzbekistan to Build and Modernize 16 Pumping Stations in Namangan Region

A new project in Uzbekistan's Namangan region aims to improve water supply for agricultural lands by building and reconstructing 16 pumping stations. Eight new stations will be constructed, and eight existing ones will be modernized with the involvement of Chinese specialists. This initiative seeks to enhance irrigation efficiency.
