Tajikistan Notifies UN of Withdrawal from International Criminal Court

Tajikistan officially notified the UN Secretary-General of its withdrawal from the Rome Statute, the treaty establishing the International Criminal Court (ICC), on November 20, 2026. The withdrawal will take effect in one year. Tajikistan signed the statute in 1998 and ratified it in 2000.
