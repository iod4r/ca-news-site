Jordan and Iraq Ban Telegram Messenger Over Security Concerns

Jordan and Iraq have announced the blocking of the Telegram messenger due to security concerns and insufficient measures against inappropriate and criminal content. Jordan's Public Security Directorate cited efforts to combat extremism, while Iraq's media organization aims to compel Telegram to review content related to the country and prevent violations. Telegram has been given 30 days to comply with Iraqi laws.
