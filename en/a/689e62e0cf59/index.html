170 Tons of Cotton Burn in Turkestan Region Factory

A fire broke out at a cotton processing plant in the Turkestan region of Kazakhstan, consuming 170 tons of cotton across 242 square meters. The Ministry of Emergency Situations reported that the fire was completely extinguished. There were no casualties, and the cause of the fire is under investigation.
