Kazakhstan Offers Three Pension Fund Investment Strategies to Contributors

Kazakhstanis can now choose from three investment strategies for their pension savings managed by private companies. Contributors can select conservative, balanced, or high-risk portfolios based on their proximity to retirement and risk tolerance. Previously, all clients' funds were invested in a single common portfolio.
