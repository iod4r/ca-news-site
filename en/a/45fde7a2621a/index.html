White House Lists Uzbekistan Among Countries at High Risk for Illicit Transit of Chinese Goods

The White House has identified Uzbekistan as a country with a high risk of illicit transit of Chinese goods to circumvent U.S. tariffs, according to a report titled "The Great Transshipment Scam." The report notes that exporters may exploit tariff differences by rerouting goods through countries with lower tariffs before they reach the American market.
