Kyrgyzstan sports federations to undergo re-accreditation and performance review

Kyrgyzstan plans to tighten requirements for sports federations and revise state funding approaches. Organizations failing to show results may face reduced support, according to the Director of the State Agency for Physical Culture and Sports. Changes to legislation mandate re-accreditation, with increased control over their activities and performance evaluation. Federations will be assessed on infrastructure, coaches, athletes, and regional presence.
