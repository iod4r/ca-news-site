Russian Oil Refining Drops 30% Amidst Wave of Drone Attacks on Refineries

Russia's oil refining capacity has decreased by approximately 30% following a series of drone attacks on major refineries since late October. Nine out of 35 large refineries, with a combined capacity of 2.2 million barrels per day, have been affected. These attacks disrupt the domestic fuel market and pose risks to oil product exports.
