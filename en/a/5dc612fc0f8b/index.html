Uzbekistan's Annual Inflation Accelerates to 6.5% in October

Uzbekistan's consumer prices rose by 0.6% in October, leading to an annual inflation rate of 6.5%, up from 6.4% in September. Food prices increased by 1% monthly and 6.4% annually. Non-food items saw a 0.2% monthly rise and 5.6% annually, while services increased by 0.6% monthly and 7.3% annually.
