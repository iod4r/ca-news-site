Kazakhstan Proposes Ethical AI Code and Climate Change Initiatives at Religious Leaders' Congress

Kazakhstan hosted the XXIV Secretariat meeting of the Congress of Leaders of World and Traditional Religions in Astana. Key outcomes include the adoption of documents on the role of religions in combating climate change and an ethical code for AI use in religion. The 'Charter of Rights for Terminally Ill Children' was also presented and supported.
