Pakistan Links Taliban Talks to Action Against Militants Operating from Afghanistan

Pakistan's Foreign Ministry stated that any dialogue with the Taliban hinges on concrete, verifiable action against armed groups operating from Afghanistan. Spokesperson Sajjad Haider Khan emphasized that talks must yield tangible security results. Pakistan also noted its contacts with Russia regarding Afghanistan but was unaware of a formal Russian mediation initiative.
