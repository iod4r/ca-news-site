Uzbekistan Energy Minister Reviews Progress on Solar Power and Energy Storage Project

Uzbekistan's Energy Minister Sherzod Khodjaev inspected the construction of a solar power plant and energy storage system in Nurabad district, Samarkand region. The project, a collaboration with Saudi company ACWA, aims for a 1000 MW solar capacity and a 1336 MW·h storage system. The first 100 MW phase is operational, with full completion expected by the end of 2027.
