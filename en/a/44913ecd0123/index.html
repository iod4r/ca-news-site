Kazakhstan Links Nuclear Power Plants to Artificial Intelligence Development Needs

Kazakhstan's President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev stated that the country plans to build several nuclear power plants to meet the growing electricity demand driven by artificial intelligence development. He noted that AI requires significant computing power, increasing the load on energy systems. Kazakhstan is already building its first nuclear plant with Russia.
