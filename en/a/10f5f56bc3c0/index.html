Kazakhstan plans mandatory housing insurance against natural disasters

Kazakhstan is planning to introduce mandatory housing insurance against natural disasters like earthquakes, floods, and wildfires. The annual contribution is estimated to range from 2,000 to 20,000 tenge, varying by region. This new system aims to shift some recovery costs from the state and individuals to an insurance mechanism. Payments upon a claim will be 500 times the contribution, ranging from one million to ten million tenge.
