Kazakhstan's Air Cargo to Receive First Airbus A340 Freighter in November

Kazakhstan's national cargo carrier, Air Cargo, is set to receive its first Airbus A340 freighter aircraft in November. The airline plans to expand its fleet to ten A340s, with a second aircraft expected in March. This initiative is part of a broader plan to modernize ground infrastructure and enhance air transit potential between Europe and Asia.
