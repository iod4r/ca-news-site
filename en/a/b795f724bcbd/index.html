Kazakhstan Conducts First Transit Oil Shipment to Kyrgyzstan

Kazakhstan has successfully completed its first transit oil shipment of 35,000 tons to Kyrgyzstan via its territory. The oil was transported through the main pipeline system to the 'Shagyr' oil-loading point and then transferred to railway tank cars for delivery. This marks a new transit route for oil products to Kyrgyzstan.
