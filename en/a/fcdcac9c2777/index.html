Kazakhstan's Energy System Forecasts Record Winter Load of 18,200 MW

Kazakhstan's Ministry of Energy forecasts a record electricity demand of 18,200 MW this winter, a 3% increase from the previous season. Energy Minister Yerlan Akkengzhenov reported that electricity generation for the first 10 months reached 102 billion kWh, up 2.3% year-on-year. The ministry also noted reduced wear on power plants and networks, with technological disruptions decreasing by an average of 6%.
