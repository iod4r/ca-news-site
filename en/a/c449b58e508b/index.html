Kazakh government rejects proposal for uniform tax rate for entrepreneurs

The Kazakh government has rejected a proposal to introduce a uniform 2-3% tax rate for entrepreneurs under a simplified declaration regime. The proposal, which also sought to remove regional authorities' power to adjust tax rates, was deemed incompatible with the new Tax Code's provisions for differentiated rates based on business activity and location.
