Turkey Approves Joint Defence Pact with Pakistan and Saudi Arabia

Turkey's parliament has approved a joint defence agreement with Pakistan and Saudi Arabia, establishing a framework for closer military cooperation and collective security. The Mecca Joint Defence Agreement commits members to treating an external attack against one as an attack against all. It includes intelligence sharing, joint military exercises, and counterterrorism cooperation.
