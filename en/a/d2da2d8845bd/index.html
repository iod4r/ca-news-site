Uzbekistan to Link Jet Fuel Prices to Global Rates, Offer New Airline Incentives

Starting in 2027, Uzbekistan will set jet fuel prices based on global averages, exempting them from excise tax and applying a zero VAT rate. Airlines will also benefit from extended customs duty exemptions on spare parts until 2040 and zero VAT on aircraft leases. These measures aim to expand the national fleet to 250 aircraft by 2030.
