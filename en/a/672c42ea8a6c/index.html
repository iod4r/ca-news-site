Kazakhstan Launches Decree on People's Digital Economy Development

President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev has signed a decree to develop Kazakhstan's people's digital economy. The initiative aims to transform the labor market and e-commerce, simplify legal frameworks for platform employment, and connect rural producers to marketplaces. Recommendations from AI expert Li Kaifu on practical AI skills training are to be incorporated.
