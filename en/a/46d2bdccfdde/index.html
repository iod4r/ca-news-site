Uzbekistan and Singapore discuss strategic partnership and innovation program

Uzbekistan and Singapore are discussing the development of a medium-term joint program on strategic and innovative partnership, focusing on trade, investment, technology transfer, public administration modernization, human capital development, and digitalization. The discussion occurred during a meeting between President Shavkat Mirziyoyev and Singapore's Senior Minister Lee Hsien Loong.
