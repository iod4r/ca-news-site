Tajikistan Signs Agreements with EBRD for 62 Million Euros for Kulob Water Supply and Road Repair

Tajikistan's Ministry of Finance and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD) signed agreements totaling 62.085 million euros for two projects. One project, costing 9.9 million euros, will modernize the water supply and sanitation system in Kulob. The second project, valued at 52.185 million euros, will restore the Baljuvon-Sari-Khosor road.
