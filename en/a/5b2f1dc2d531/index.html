Fergana Region's Economy Shows Growth: GDP Up 9.1%, Investment Reaches 25.7 Trillion Sums

Fergana Region's Gross Regional Product (GRP) reached 65.2 trillion sums in January-June 2026, a 9.1% increase year-on-year. The region's share in Uzbekistan's GDP rose to 6.1%. Services dominate the GRP at 49.2%, followed by agriculture (27.3%), industry (15.7%), and construction (7.8%). Investments in the region's economy and social sphere totaled 25.7 trillion sums.
