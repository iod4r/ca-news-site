Residents of Uzbek Village Transferred to Kyrgyzstan Receive Property Documents

Residents of the village of Kara-Zhantak, previously part of Uzbekistan's Fergana region, have received property documents for 295 homes. The settlement officially transferred to Kyrgyzstan's jurisdiction in 2024 following border demarcation. Local authorities are also developing infrastructure, including a new water pipeline and plans for social facilities like a school and kindergarten.
