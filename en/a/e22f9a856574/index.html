Kyrgyzstan introduces new requirements for waste recycling businesses

Businesses in Kyrgyzstan involved in waste recycling must be registered with the state enterprise 'Eco Operator' under the Ministry of Environment. Producers and importers are reminded of their annual obligation to report on waste recycling quotas. Companies handling hazardous waste (classes I-V) may also require a specific license. 'Eco Operator' manages the waste management system and collects recycling fees.
