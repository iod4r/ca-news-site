Turkmenistan Customs Halts Parcel Deliveries After Discovering Prohibited Substances

Turkmenistan's State Customs Service has suspended the release of international parcels for about a week. The halt follows the discovery of a large quantity of tramadol, a prohibited opioid painkiller, in a shipment believed to have arrived from Turkey. Authorities reportedly plan to broadcast the seizure and involved individuals on state television before resuming deliveries.
