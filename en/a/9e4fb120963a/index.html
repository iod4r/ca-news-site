New Oilseed Plant to Be Constructed in Turkmenistan's Dashoguz Region

Turkmenistan's President has signed a decree for the construction of a new oilseed processing plant in the Garashsyzlyk district of Dashoguz region. The plant will have an annual capacity to process 60,000 tons of industrial cotton seeds. This initiative aims to develop the agricultural sector and ensure food security.
