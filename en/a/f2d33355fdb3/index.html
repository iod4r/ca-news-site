Turkmenistan's TAPI Gas Pipeline Nears Afghan City, Commissioning by End of 2026

Turkmenistan announced the TAPI gas pipeline has reached the outskirts of Herat, Afghanistan, and is expected to be commissioned by the end of 2026. Satellite images confirm the pipeline extends from Turkmenistan's border to a construction zone west of Herat. The project, envisioned in the 1990s, aims to connect Turkmenistan's gas fields to Afghanistan, Pakistan, and India.
