Kyrgyzstan: Election campaign to begin December 23, Central Election Commission states

In Kyrgyzstan, presidential candidates can begin their election campaign on December 23, after official registration. The Central Election Commission will monitor compliance with campaign laws. Violations can be reported to the commission, which has working groups to review complaints and make decisions. The presidential election is scheduled for January 27, 2027.
