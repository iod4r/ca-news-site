New Apple Juice Concentrate Plant Opens in Jalal-Abad Region

A new apple juice concentrate plant, owned by "Global Eco Green" LLC, has been launched in the Alabuka district of the Jalal-Abad region. The facility can process up to 500 tons of apple concentrate daily and is expected to create up to 100 new jobs. The plant aims to help farmers sell their produce and promote local processing.
