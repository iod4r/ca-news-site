Kazakhstan Hosts Media Forum on Oil and Gas Industry Coverage

A national media forum, Media Energy Talks, was held in Astana to discuss the coverage of the oil and gas sector. The event, attended by the Deputy Minister of Energy, explored how to report on complex technological and economic processes, and the impact of AI and social media on journalism. Masterclasses on oilfield services and fuel markets were also conducted.
