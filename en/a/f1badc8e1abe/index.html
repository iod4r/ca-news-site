Contractors to Face Penalties for Delays in Almaty's TEC-3 Modernization

Samruk-Energy announced that contractors for Almaty's TEC-3 modernization project will face penalties for delays, though the project's cost will not increase. The completion date has been pushed back by a year, with the project only 26% complete as of July. The former chairman of Samruk-Energy was dismissed in October due to unsatisfactory project implementation and lack of oversight on construction deadlines.
