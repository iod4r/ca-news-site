Uzbekistan Aims for 54% Green Energy by 2030, Launches New Projects

Uzbekistan plans to increase the share of "green" energy to 54% by 2030, with potential for higher achievement at current rates. Energy Minister Sherzod Khodjayev announced the launch of five new energy facilities worth over 20 trillion Uzbek soums. These include Central Asia's largest thermal power station and two major energy storage systems. A combined solar power and storage project was launched in Nurota district.
