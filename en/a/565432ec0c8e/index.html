First Natural Saxaul Sprouts Appear in Aral Sea Desert Following Reforestation Efforts

The first natural saxaul sprouts have appeared in the desert on the dried bed of the Aral Sea, where saplings have been planted since 2021. Over 3,440 tons of saxaul seeds and other salt-tolerant plants have been sown, with over 53 million saxaul seedlings planted. This initiative aims to restore vegetation cover and create conditions for livestock development.
