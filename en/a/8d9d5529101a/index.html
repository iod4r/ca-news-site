Uzbekistan Recovers $850,000 in Wages for Citizens Working Abroad

Uzbekistan's Migration Agency has recovered $850,000 in unpaid wages and compensation for 817 citizens working abroad during the first nine months of 2026. The majority of cases involved workers in Russia, followed by Turkey and South Korea. The agency also facilitated the return of identification documents for 532 citizens. Citizens facing difficulties abroad are encouraged to contact the agency.
