Kazakhstan to Invest Trillions of Tenge in Transit Corridor Upgrades

Kazakhstan plans to allocate over 2 trillion tenge for the development of transit road corridors within the next three years. The Ministry of Transport detailed plans to improve routes like "Aktobe – Kyzylorda" and "Beyneu – Saksaulsky." Additionally, 37 border crossing points within the EAEU will be modernized by 2027 to double throughput capacity.
