Kazakh Official Punished for Hindering Investor in Abai Region

A government official in Kazakhstan's Abai Region has been disciplined for obstructing an investor. The prosecutor's office intervened after discovering discrepancies in property registration for a sunflower oil processing plant in Semey. The delays prevented the investor from registering the facility and beginning operations. Following the prosecutor's action, the information system was updated, allowing the investor to proceed.
