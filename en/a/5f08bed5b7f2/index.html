Kyrgyz journalists Bolot Temirov and Makhabat Tajibek kyzy receive international The Albies award

Investigative journalist Bolot Temirov and Temirov Live project head Makhabat Tajibek kyzy from Kyrgyzstan have been named laureates of the international The Albies award. The award is presented by the Clooney Foundation for Justice. Temirov stated this on social media, noting the award recognizes the work of projects whose materials were deemed extremist in Kyrgyzstan. The ceremony will be held in London on October 7.
