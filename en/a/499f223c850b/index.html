AI to Enhance Protection of Kazakhstan's Energy Facilities Against Drone Attacks

Artificial intelligence will be used to protect Kazakhstan's energy facilities from drone attacks, according to the Ministry of Energy. AI applications include cybersecurity, threat detection, and physical security, such as detecting and suppressing drones. The ACR Center is being established to manage these cyber-physical resilience efforts in the fuel and energy complex.
