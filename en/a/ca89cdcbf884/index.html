Russia significantly increases diesel fuel supplies to Kyrgyzstan in August

Russia's diesel fuel supplies to Kyrgyzstan surged more than 16-fold in August, reaching over 72,000 tonnes compared to 4,400 tonnes in July. This increase is attributed to a recovery in Russian diesel production. Overall, Russia exported over 370,000 tonnes of diesel to four Central Asian countries and Mongolia in August.
