Turkmenistan Imports Block Meat and Live Cattle to Address Meat Shortage

Turkmenistan is importing block meat and live cattle to alleviate a growing meat deficit. New, cheaper meat, including buffalo meat, is appearing in state stores and markets in Ashgabat and Kipchak. This follows months of rising prices and public discontent, with some citizens detained for protesting high costs.
