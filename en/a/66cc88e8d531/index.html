Kazakhstan Sets November 29, 2026, for Snap Elections of Maslikhat Deputies

Kazakhstan's Central Election Commission has scheduled snap elections for maslikhat (local representative body) deputies across all levels for November 29, 2026. This follows the early termination of the current maslikhats' powers by President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev. A total of 3,447 deputies will be elected for five-year terms.
