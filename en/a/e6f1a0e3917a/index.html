Turkmenistan Authorities Remove Satellite Dishes, Seek Starlink Users

Authorities in Balkanabat, Turkmenistan, have resumed efforts to remove satellite dishes used for internet and television. This is part of a campaign to identify users of the Starlink satellite internet system in a country with poor internet infrastructure. Police are targeting individuals, particularly ethnic Kazakhs, who may have obtained Starlink equipment from neighboring Kazakhstan.
