Iran's IRGC claims attack on US Patriot radars in Bahrain

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) claims to have attacked two US Patriot air defence radars at bases in Bahrain. The IRGC announced this claim on Sunday, November 22. Bahrain has condemned alleged Iranian drone attacks on its territory, stating they hit a civilian facility and caused damage. The US military has not confirmed or denied the claim.
