Kyrgyzstan Projects Significant Increase in State Company Dividend Revenue

Kyrgyzstan has sharply increased its forecast for dividend revenue from state-owned companies, expecting 55.25 billion soms by 2026, a substantial rise from the initial 22.8 billion soms. For the first nine months of 2026, the state has already received 54.2 billion soms, nearing 98% of the revised annual target. This represents a near doubling of revenue compared to the previous year.
