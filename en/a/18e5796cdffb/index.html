Uzbekistan and Turkey Expand Cooperation in Coal Field Development

Uzbekistan and Turkey are set to enhance cooperation in developing the country's coal resources. Officials from Uzbekistan's Ministry of Mining Industry and Geology met with the regional head of Turkey's Cengiz Holding to discuss investment projects and the geological exploration of promising coal deposits in the Surkhandarya and Tashkent regions. This collaboration aims to boost foreign investment and develop new projects.
