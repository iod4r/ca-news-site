Uzbekistan Proposes Opening UN Counter-Terrorism Regional Office

Uzbekistan proposes establishing a regional office of the UN Counter-Terrorism Committee to prevent terrorist threats, including those using AI. President Shavkat Mirziyoyev made the initiative at a meeting in Astana within the Aqaba Process. The office would support Central Asian countries in detecting terrorist content, financing, and utilizing AI for counter-terrorism efforts.
