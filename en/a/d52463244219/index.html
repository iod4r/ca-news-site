Rosatom to Begin Construction of Two Large Power Units in Uzbekistan in 2027

Rosatom plans to commence the full-scale construction of two large power units, each with a capacity of 1,000 megawatts, in Uzbekistan by 2027. Currently, work is focused on two small modular reactors of 55 megawatts each. Engineering surveys and design refinements are underway for the larger units, with active work expected to begin next year.
