Kazakhstan MP Proposes Real-Time Location Tracking for Servicemen During Exercises

A Member of Parliament in Kazakhstan has proposed equipping military personnel with geolocation devices during exercises. The devices would provide real-time location tracking and an SOS function in emergencies. The proposal also includes drone surveillance and continuous weather data transmission to a central point to enhance safety during training.
