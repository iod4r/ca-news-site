Kazakh President Orders State Commission to Oversee 'People's Construction' Program Funds

President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev has ordered the creation of a state commission to monitor the spending of budget funds for the 'Narodnaya Stroyka' ('People's Construction') program. Announced in his October 28 address, the program aims to develop regions and improve citizens' well-being. The commission will include government officials, lawmakers, and public figures.
