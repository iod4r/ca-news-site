Kazakhstan Reports Over 9,000 Arrests for Theft in 10 Months, Crime Groups Dismantled

Kazakhstan's Ministry of Internal Affairs reported a 18.4% decrease in overall thefts and a 23.3% drop in burglaries during the first 10 months of the year. Over 9,000 individuals were detained, and 1,248 criminal groups were neutralized. The ministry highlighted successful operations in Taraz and Turkestan region, recovering stolen valuables.
