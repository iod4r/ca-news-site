Kazakhstan Discusses Religious Education Cooperation with Al-Azhar

Kazakhstan's Vice President Yerlan Karin met with Abdulrahman Musa, advisor to the Grand Imam of Al-Azhar, to discuss cooperation in religious education and interfaith dialogue. The meeting occurred during the XXIV session of the Secretariat of the Congress of Leaders of World and Traditional Religions. Discussions also covered collaboration with other religious organizations.
