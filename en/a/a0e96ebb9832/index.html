Videographer Admits Aiding in Granite Tile Theft, Denies Pressure

Ulan Nusipkozha, videographer for activist Sanjar Bokayev, admitted to aiding in the theft of granite tiles in Almaty but denied being an organizer or perpetrator. He testified that Bokayev instructed him to film the removal of tiles from a construction site. Nusipkozha claimed he suspected theft and that Bokayev later ordered the tiles returned.
