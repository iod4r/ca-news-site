Kyrgyzstan Remains Georgia's Top Market Despite 40.7% Export Drop

Kyrgyzstan maintained its position as Georgia's largest export market in January-October 2026, despite a 40.7% decrease in exports to $731.5 million. Cars accounted for over 95% of this trade. Georgia's overall exports grew by 17.9% during the same period.
