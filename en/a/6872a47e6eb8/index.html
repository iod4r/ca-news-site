Kazakhstan Introduces Electronic Prescriptions for High-Risk Medications

Kazakhstan is implementing mandatory electronic prescriptions for high-risk medications, linked to a digital tracking system. Since the start of 2026, authorities have issued fines exceeding 8.2 million tenge for over-the-counter antibiotic sales. Common antibiotics like Azithromycin and Ciprofloxacin are frequently sold without prescriptions.
