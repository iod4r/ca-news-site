COP31 Climate Talks Collapse as EU and Others Reject Final Draft Deal

UN climate talks at COP31 collapsed Sunday after the European Union and other nations rejected the final draft agreement, deeming it too weak to combat global warming. The proposed deal had acknowledged projected temperature overshoot and called for faster development of carbon-removal technologies. Disagreements over fuels and climate finance were cited as dealbreakers.
