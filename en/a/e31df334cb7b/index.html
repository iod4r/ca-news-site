Over 900 Afghan Migrants Return to the Country Through Border Crossings

The Taliban announced that 971 Afghan migrants, including 219 families, returned to Afghanistan through various border crossings on Friday. Hamdullah Fitrat, a Taliban spokesperson, provided figures for returns via Torkham, Pul-e Abrishum, Spin Boldak, and Islam Qala. UN and aid groups expressed concern about the impact on Afghanistan's capacity.
