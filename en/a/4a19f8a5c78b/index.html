Kazakhstan Recovers 617 Million Tenge for Cybercrime Victims

Over nine months, Kazakhstan blocked 5.5 billion tenge to combat cybercrime. Victims have voluntarily recovered 617 million tenge, according to the Ministry of Internal Affairs. The Ministry collaborates with the National Bank's Anti-Fraud Center to identify suspicious transactions and halt the movement of stolen funds. Over 76,000 incidents were initiated by law enforcement agencies.
