OIC Reaffirms Support for Girls' Education and Aid to Afghanistan

The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) has reaffirmed its commitment to supporting girls' education and providing humanitarian and economic assistance to Afghanistan. At a meeting in Doha, the OIC emphasized counterterrorism, nutrition, and food security as priorities. Cooperation with the UN, World Bank, and EU is deemed essential for coordinating aid efforts amid Afghanistan's ongoing humanitarian and economic challenges.
