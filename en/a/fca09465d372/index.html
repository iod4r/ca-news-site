East Kazakhstan Enterprise Fined Over 100 Million Tenge for Environmental Violations

An enterprise in East Kazakhstan has been fined over 100 million tenge for environmental violations. The Department of Ecology conducted an unscheduled inspection of TOO "Vostoktsvetmet" and found it failed to address consequences of subsoil use at the "Yubileyno-Snegiikhinskoye" deposit. The company was fined and ordered to rectify the situation.
