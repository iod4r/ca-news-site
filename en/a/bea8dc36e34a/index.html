Uzbekistan to Launch Electronic System for Medical Outsourcing Oversight by April 2027

Uzbekistan will launch an electronic system called "Medical Outsourcing" by April 1, 2027, to track services transferred from state hospitals to private companies. The system aims to monitor contract fulfillment and quality, with a goal to increase outsourced clinical and auxiliary services to 30% by 2030.
