Three Mobile Operators Fined in Kostanay for Exceeding Signal Levels in Prisons

Three mobile operators, 'Kar-Tel', 'Kcell', and 'Mobile Telecom-Service', have been fined in the Kostanay region for exceeding permitted radio signal levels within correctional facilities. This violation was discovered during a special inspection of the penal system. Previously, a prisoner was prosecuted for fraud committed via mobile phone.
