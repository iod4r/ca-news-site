Kazakhstan Allocates 35.8 Billion Tenge for Upcoming Maslikhat Elections

Kazakhstan has allocated 35.8 billion tenge for the upcoming snap elections of maslikhat deputies scheduled for November 29. Approximately 70% of the funds, totaling 25.1 billion tenge, will be used to pay election commission members. The elections will be held in 3,113 single-mandate districts with 10,971 registered candidates.
