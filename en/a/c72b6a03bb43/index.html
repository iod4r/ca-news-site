Almaty's TETs-3 Modernization Completion Delayed by One Year to 2027

The completion of modernization for Almaty's TETs-3 has been postponed by one year, with work now expected to finish by the end of 2027. This delay was announced by the Minister of Energy, Yerlan Akkenzhenov, who noted that the project is not progressing well. TETs-2's heating section is complete, and its electrical part will be finished by November.
