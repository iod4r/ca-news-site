Kazakhstan Prosecutors Introduce New School Deputies to Combat Bullying

Following reports of widespread bullying and extortion in Kazakhstan's Zhambyl region, prosecutors have introduced the position of deputy principal for legal affairs in 190 schools. This initiative aims to reduce juvenile crime and improve how schools handle violence, addressing issues of underreporting and institutional reputation concerns.
