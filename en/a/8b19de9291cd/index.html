Russian Rapper Navai Arrested in Kazakhstan on Drug Smuggling Charges

Russian rapper Navai (Navai Bahman oglu Bakirov) has been arrested for two months in Kazakhstan on suspicion of drug smuggling. He was detained on October 13th while entering Kazakhstan from Kyrgyzstan. Police found and seized drugs during a search of his vehicle at the 'Korday' border crossing.
