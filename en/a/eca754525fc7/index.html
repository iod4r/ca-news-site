Kyrgyzstan: 47.4 Hectares of Private Land to be Transferred to Presidential Affairs Department

In Kara-Suu, Osh region, Kyrgyzstan, 47.4 hectares of privately owned land will be transferred for indefinite use to the Presidential Affairs Department. The land will be exchanged with owners' consent for plots from the State Agricultural Land Fund. The purpose of the land transfer is not specified.
