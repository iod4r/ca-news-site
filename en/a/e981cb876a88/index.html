Lithuania Imposes Restrictions on Central Asian Migrants, Visa Centers to Halt Applications

Lithuania has introduced new restrictions affecting approximately 29,000 Central Asian citizens residing in the country, primarily labor migrants. Visa centers in Kyrgyzstan and Uzbekistan have stopped accepting work permit applications. Starting November 1, visa centers in Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, and Kazakhstan will cease accepting applications for long-term Lithuanian residency.
