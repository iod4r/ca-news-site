New Kazakhstan Entry Rules: Kyrgyz Citizens Can Still Travel Freely

Kazakhstan is implementing new digital migration mechanisms, including a preliminary entry approval system (QazETA) and a unified information database. While the Ministry of Internal Affairs plans a phased rollout from August to December 2026, potentially with service fees, Kyrgyzstan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs clarified that Kyrgyz citizens currently do not require preliminary approval for travel to Kazakhstan. Discussions are underway to analyze EAEU agreements.
