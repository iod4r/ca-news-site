AI Model of World Ocean to Be Created for Climate Change Studies

A Chilean center of the French Institute of Digital Science and Technology has received $2.5 million from an American company to build an AI model of the world's oceans. The model will help predict climate change impacts more accurately by learning from physical, biological, and genomic data. It aims to forecast changes in marine life and provide early warnings for harmful algal blooms and coral bleaching.
