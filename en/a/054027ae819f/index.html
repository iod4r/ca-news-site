Uzbekistan Central Bank Launches System for Direct Salary Payments to Any Bank Card

Uzbek citizens can now receive salaries and bonuses directly to any bank card or e-wallet, regardless of their employer's bank. The Central Bank has introduced the "Funds Distribution Center" system to facilitate this. Previously, employees had to use cards from their employer's designated bank. This new system initially targets private sector organizations and their employees, with plans for future expansion.
