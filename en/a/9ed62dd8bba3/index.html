Kyrgyzstan's berry exports to Russia's Kirov region double

Berry exports from Kyrgyzstan to Russia's Kirov region have doubled in the first eight months of 2026, reaching 50.7 tons. This increase is attributed to favorable weather conditions in Kyrgyzstan, more stable and cheaper logistics, and competitive pricing by Kyrgyz producers. No quarantine pests were found in the shipments.
