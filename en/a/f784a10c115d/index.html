New Industrial Enterprises Launched in Dushanbe, Tajikistan

Tajikistan's President Emomali Rahmon and the Mayor of Dushanbe attended the launch of two industrial enterprises in the Shokhmanusur district. These include a gas block production plant, ООО «Эко Блок», aiming for 300,000 cubic meters annually, and a beverage and confectionery production facility, ООО «Шахдоб». The gas block plant is noted as the largest of its kind in Tajikistan.
