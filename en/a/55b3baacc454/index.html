Truck Queues Ease at Kazakhstan-Uzbekistan Border Crossing, State Revenue Committee Reports

Long truck queues at the 'Bawyrzhan Konysbayev' border crossing between Kazakhstan's Turkestan region and Uzbekistan have reportedly shortened. While a viral video showed a 40km queue, the State Revenue Committee stated around 300 trucks were waiting, with over 700 trucks typically crossing daily. This crossing is a key transit route.
