Kazakhstan to Establish Commission to Oversee Public Construction Project Budget

Kazakhstan's President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev announced the creation of a state commission to strictly monitor the spending of the 'Public Construction' program budget. The commission will include heads of sectoral departments, parliament deputies, and public figures. The president declared the upcoming year a year of nationwide construction.
