Taliban spokesman: Pakistani airstrikes killed four civilians in Afghanistan

Taliban spokesman Zabihullah Mujahid stated that four civilians were killed in Pakistani airstrikes on Kandahar, Paktia, and Khost provinces. He condemned the attacks as a "blatant act of aggression" and vowed they would not go unanswered. Pakistan's information minister claimed the strikes were in response to drones entering Pakistani territory.
