Ekibastuz GRES-1 fined for environmental pollution from ash and slag waste

Pavlodar environmental prosecutor's office discovered environmental pollution from ash and slag waste near Ekibastuz GRES-1 due to a leak. The enterprise was fined 500 MCI (2,162,500 tenge) by the Ekibastuz City Court for violating environmental laws. Another fine of approximately 70 million tenge was issued for exceeding atmospheric emission limits.
