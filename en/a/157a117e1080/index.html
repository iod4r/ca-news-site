Uzbekistan Plans to Increase Electricity Generation Capacity to 46.1 GW by 2030

Uzbekistan aims to reach 46.1 GW of electricity generation capacity by 2030, producing nearly 129 billion kWh annually. By 2035, capacity is projected to hit 57.2 GW, generating 154.7 billion kWh. These plans were presented to President Mirziyoyev, considering economic growth and rising energy consumption.
