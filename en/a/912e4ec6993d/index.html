Tajikistan's Minister of Transport Inspects Baljuvon-Sari Khosor Road Reconstruction

Tajikistan's Minister of Transport, Azim Ibrahim, visited the first phase of the Baljuvon-Sari Khosor road reconstruction. The project involves 115 workers, including 40 Chinese nationals and 75 locals. The 54.1 km road will feature 10 bridges and 121 culverts, funded by the EBRD and built by a Chinese company.
