Israel Restricts Palestinian Workers' Entry to West Bank Ahead of Elections

The Israeli military announced that Palestinian workers with permits will be barred from entering Israeli settlements in the occupied West Bank on Sunday and Monday. Restrictions also apply to industrial zones. On election day, all entry and exit from the West Bank and Jordan Valley will be blocked, with land crossings closed. Settlers have also reportedly prevented farmers from harvesting olives.
