Tajikistan Imposes Moratorium on Small and Medium Business Inspections Until 2029

Tajikistan's President Emomali Rahmon has signed a decree imposing a moratorium on all types of inspections for small and medium-sized businesses until January 1, 2029. The decree, excluding tax authority inspections, aims to support entrepreneurship, strengthen industrial and export potential, and create favorable conditions for business development and investment. Previous moratoria have positively impacted business growth and job creation.
