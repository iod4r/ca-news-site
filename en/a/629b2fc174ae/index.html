Almaty Plans High-Rise Development in Designated Zone Amidst Population Growth

Almaty's chief planner, Askat Sadykov, announced plans for high-rise construction in a specific zone near Sayran to accommodate population growth and land scarcity. While most of the city will retain its current low-rise structure, a new polycentric development model will be implemented in new areas. The city's population is projected to reach 3.6 million by 2040.
