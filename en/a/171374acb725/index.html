Kazakhstan to Allow Private Firms to Manage Up to 30% of Pension Savings

Kazakhstan plans to transfer up to 30% of citizens' pension savings to private investment companies for management. This initiative is part of a capital market development program aiming to reform the pension asset management model. Initially, a pilot program will allow limited transfer of liquid assets to qualified investment organizations.
