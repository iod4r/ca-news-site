Kazakhstan and China establish joint investment fund for new projects

Kazakhstan and China are establishing a specialized investment fund to finance new projects in Kazakhstan, operating through the Astana International Financial Centre. An agreement was signed by Kazakh Invest and Silk Road Finance Corporation at the Kazakhstan-China Investment Forum in Almaty. Bilateral trade reached $48.7 billion in 2025.
