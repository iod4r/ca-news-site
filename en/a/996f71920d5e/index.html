Search Underway for Foreign National Missing in Almaty Mountains for Three Days

A 73-year-old South Korean national, Cho Kuk-hee, has been missing in the Almaty Mountains for three days. The tourist disappeared on September 28 after ascending to an altitude of approximately 3200 meters. Search and rescue operations involving around 50 specialists, 8 vehicles, 7 drones, 3 canine units, and a helicopter are ongoing. The Ministry of Emergency Situations reported that operations are continuing around the clock.
