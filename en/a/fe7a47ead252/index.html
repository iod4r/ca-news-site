Kazakhstan's Interior Ministry Denies Cybercrime Incidents on National Messenger Aitu

Kazakhstan's Ministry of Internal Affairs has stated that no cybercrime incidents have been registered on the national messenger platform Aitu. The ministry's cybercrime department chief, Beibit Birzhanov, confirmed this, noting that the platform's architecture provides a high level of security. Previously, regulators had reported that Aitu's servers and data are within Kazakhstan's secure network, preventing cross-border data transfer risks.
