Kyrgyzstan's Electricity Consumption Rises Nearly 30 Percent Annually

Electricity consumption in Kyrgyzstan has significantly increased, with daily usage up by 29.1 percent compared to the same period last year, according to the National Electric Network of Kyrgyzstan. The republic's current daily consumption stands at 66.022 million kilowatt-hours. The company urges citizens to use electricity rationally and conserve resources.
