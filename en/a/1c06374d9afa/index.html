21 State Procurements Canceled in Stepnogorsk After Prosecutor's Review

In Stepnogorsk, 21 state procurement procedures totaling 1.8 billion tenge were canceled following a prosecutor's review. Violations included inflated costs, excessive volumes, and unnecessary work in city maintenance plans. The prosecutor's office intervened, leading to the preservation of 714 million tenge in budget funds.
