Kazakhstan begins construction of 8.7 billion tenge reservoir in West Kazakhstan region

Kazakhstan is constructing a reservoir with a volume of 28.4 million cubic meters on the Bolshoy Uzen River in the West Kazakhstan region. The project, supported by the Islamic Development Bank, aims to irrigate 12,000 hectares and will cost an estimated 8.7 billion tenge. It will improve water supply and protect settlements from floods.
