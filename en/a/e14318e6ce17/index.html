Kazakhstan Introduces SMS System for Reporting Fake Vaccination Records

Kazakhstan has launched an SMS service allowing citizens to verify vaccination records and report discrepancies. After vaccination, individuals will receive an SMS from 1414; replying with '811' confirms the record, while '812' indicates a false entry. This aims to identify and correct inaccuracies in the vaccination database.
