Kazakh President Tokayev Proposes State Commission to Oversee Public Construction Program Funds

Kazakh President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev has proposed establishing a state commission to monitor the spending of budget funds allocated for the "Public Construction" program. This initiative, announced in his October 28 address, aims to ensure strict oversight of the program's implementation. The commission will include various officials, deputies, public figures, and journalists.
