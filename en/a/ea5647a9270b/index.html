Uzbekistan's Fergana Region Aims to Double Incomes for a Quarter of Residents by 2027

Fergana Region in Uzbekistan plans to double the incomes of 1.1 million residents, approximately a quarter of its population, by 2027. The region aims to reduce unemployment to 3% and poverty to 2.7% this year. Income growth will be a key performance indicator for regional leaders.
