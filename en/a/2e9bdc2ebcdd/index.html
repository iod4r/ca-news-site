Osh Mayor's Office Auctions Car Market with Annual Rent of 326 Million Som

The Mayor's Office of Osh, Kyrgyzstan, has put the 'Osh Una Bazary' car market up for auction, seeking an annual rent of 326.38 million som. The winning bidder will gain the right to use the 12.5-hectare site, including administrative buildings and canteens, for two years. Applications are open for 29 days.
