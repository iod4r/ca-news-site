Tashkent Metro to Introduce 5-Car Trains During Peak Hours

Tashkent's metro will operate 5-car trains during peak hours following a city council decision. This aims to address overcrowding, with 85.4% of residents reporting issues during morning and evening commutes. The measure will be implemented by November 20, 2026, on key lines, reducing train intervals to 2-3 minutes.
