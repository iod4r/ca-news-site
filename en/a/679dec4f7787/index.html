Highest Salaries for Working Pensioners in Kazakhstan Found in Foreign Companies

Working pensioners in Kazakhstan aged over 65 earn the highest salaries in foreign companies, averaging over 477,000 tenge monthly. State enterprises pay an average of 313,274 tenge, while Kazakhstani private companies offer 364,105 tenge. Foreign firms' average salaries are about 52% higher than state ones.
