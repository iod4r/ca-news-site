Tajikistan Villages Explore Biogas from Cow Dung to Replace Firewood

In Tajikistan, UNDP projects have installed 37 biogas reactors converting cow dung into fuel and fertilizer, reducing greenhouse gas emissions. One family in Kulob reports lower energy costs and improved crop yields due to the biogas system, which also helps conserve land fertility amid climate change.
