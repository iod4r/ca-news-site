Kazakhstan Presents Experience in Returning Citizens from Syria and Iraq to OSCE

Kazakhstan shared its experience in the repatriation and social adaptation of citizens from Syria and Iraq at an OSCE briefing in Astana. The operations "Zhusan" and "Rusafa" successfully returned 756 individuals, including 528 children. Kazakhstan proposed establishing a regional working group to coordinate efforts for protecting affected citizens.
