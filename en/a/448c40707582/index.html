Kazakhstan Aims to Attract $115 Billion in Data Center Valley Investment Over 20 Years

Kazakhstan plans to attract $115 billion in investments for its 'Data Center Valley' project over the next 20 years. Prime Minister Olzhas Bektenov chaired a meeting on the project's progress, highlighting its potential to boost digital economy and exports. The first phase includes three data centers with a total capacity of 304 MW, with the first expected in Q2 2027.
