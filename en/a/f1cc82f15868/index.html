Kyrgyzstan to Host Sixth World Nomad Games in 2026

Kyrgyzstan will host the Sixth World Nomad Games from August 31 to September 6, 2026, expecting guests from over one hundred countries. The games will combine ethnosport, ethno-culture, traditional knowledge, and scientific/educational programs. The concept emphasizes the inseparable link between nomadic games and the history and lifestyle of the peoples who created them.
