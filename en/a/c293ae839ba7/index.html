Kazakhstan to Establish Full-Cycle Drone Production and Test Autonomous Freight

Kazakhstan has approved a comprehensive plan for 2026-2029 to develop a full cycle of drone production, including vertiports and charging stations. In 2027-2028, a pilot project for autonomous freight transport will be launched. The initiative aims to foster sovereign production from hardware to software, offering state support and incentives for competitive enterprises.
