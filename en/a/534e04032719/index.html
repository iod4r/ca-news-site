US Kok-Boru Team Honors First Coach in Kyrgyzstan

The U.S. Kok-Boru team, led by captain Ladd Howell, visited the home of their first coach, Mirlan Srazhdinov, to honor his memory. The team presented commemorative gifts to the family and fondly recalled their mentor, who facilitated connections between American and Kyrgyz players. Srazhdinov was a player and captain for the 'Börülü' team and represented Kyrgyzstan at the first World Nomad Games.
