Almaty to Spend 3.2 Billion Tenge to Extend Ryskeldi Street to Bypass Road

Almaty's akimat has announced a tender for the first phase of extending Ryskeldi Street to the Big Almaty Ring Road (BAKAD). The project's initial cost exceeds 3.2 billion tenge. This new road aims to improve traffic flow in the western part of the city and provide access to the bypass. The construction will take place in the Karasai district of Almaty region.
