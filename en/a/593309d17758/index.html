Kazakh President Proposes Observer Status for Russia in Turkic States Organization

Kazakh President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev suggested granting Russia observer status or developing cooperation within the Turkic States Organization (TSO). He cited historical ties and the presence of millions of Turkic people in Russia. Critics argue this is hypocritical given Russia's policies towards Turkic languages and activists. The Kremlin supports the idea, highlighting historical ties and the presence of Turkic peoples within Russia.
