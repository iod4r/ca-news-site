Chinese investors to inject up to $500 million into three wind farms in Akmola region

Chinese investors have signed agreements to build three wind power stations and modernize an elevator in Kazakhstan's Akmola region. The total investment for these projects could reach $506 million. The wind farms, with a combined capacity of 450 MW, are planned for construction starting in October 2026 and completion by 2028.
