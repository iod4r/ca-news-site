Kyrgyzstan Amends Funding and Procurement Rules for Special Status Universities

Kyrgyzstan's president has signed a law altering public procurement and financing procedures for higher education institutions with special status. Universities under the direct purview of the president are now excluded from special provisions, meaning their procurement will follow general legislation. The changes also affect financial operations, requiring these presidential-affiliated universities to adhere to standard budgetary system regulations.
