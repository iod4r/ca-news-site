Kyrgyzstan Extradites Suspect in $15 Million Car Fraud Case from Moldova

Kyrgyzstan has extradited a citizen from Moldova who is accused of fraud totaling over 15 million Kyrgyz soms in car sales. The individual was on an international wanted list. According to investigators, the suspect, identified as A.R., defrauded individuals by falsely promising car purchase and sale deals. The General Prosecutor's Office collaborated with Moldovan authorities for the extradition.
