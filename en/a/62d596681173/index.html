Three Hangars Built in Kyrgyzstan's Osh Region for Road Construction Equipment

Three hangars have been constructed in the Kara-Kulja district of Kyrgyzstan's Osh region to store specialized equipment for the Myrzake-Kara-Kulzha-Alaykuu highway project. The hangars, measuring 50 meters, 50 meters, and 20 meters in length, will protect machinery from adverse weather, ensuring uninterrupted road work. The total length of the highway is 138 km.
