Kazakhstan to Build Three Major Children's Medical Centers in Astana and Almaty

Kazakhstan plans to construct 20 new maternal and child healthcare facilities by 2029, including three major republican children's medical centers in Astana and Almaty. President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev announced the ambitious project, emphasizing the need for healthcare infrastructure development due to the country's rapid population growth. The new centers will enhance diagnostic and treatment capabilities for complex pediatric cases.
