King Abdullah II of Jordan to Visit Kazakhstan for Official Talks

King Abdullah II of Jordan will pay an official visit to Kazakhstan on November 5-6 at the invitation of President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev. The leaders will discuss expanding bilateral cooperation in trade, economy, investment, and cultural-humanitarian spheres. The visit aims to strengthen strategic partnership and allied relations between the two nations. The King's visit coincides with the Aqaba Process summit in Astana.
