Kyrgyzstan's Transport Minister Reports Progress on China-Kyrgyzstan-Uzbekistan Railway

The construction of the China-Kyrgyzstan-Uzbekistan railway is progressing rapidly, according to Kyrgyzstan's Minister of Transport and Communications, Talantbek Soltobaev. The project, planned for five years with completion by 2031, involves extensive tunnel work and infrastructure development. Over 10,000 workers and 7,000 units of special equipment are currently engaged.
