Butun Kyrgyzstan Party to Support Ishak Masaliev's Presidential Candidacy

The political party 'Butun Kyrgyzstan' has announced its full support for Ishak Masaliev's candidacy in the upcoming presidential elections. Party leader Adakhan Madumarov confirmed the decision. Masaliev is the fifth candidate to officially register with the Central Election Commission, with the nomination period closing on November 13. The elections are scheduled for January 27, 2027.
