Kyrgyzstan Citizen Dies from Knife Wounds in Istanbul; Compatriot Detained

A citizen of Kyrgyzstan has died from knife wounds in Istanbul, Turkey. The Consulate General of Kyrgyzstan in Istanbul reported the detention of her compatriot, who is a suspect. The incident occurred on October 5th following a conflict between the two women. The victim, born in 1991, died at the scene, while the suspect, born in 1994, has been apprehended by Turkish police.
