North Kazakhstan resident jailed for illegal lending, assets seized

A resident of North Kazakhstan has been convicted for operating an illegal lending scheme without a license, earning 774 million tenge. The court has seized six apartments acquired through these illicit activities. The man was found guilty of 78 counts of illegal lending, where he provided loans to individuals unable to obtain them from banks, often masking the transactions as property sales. The verdict is not yet final.
