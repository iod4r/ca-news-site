Kazakhstan Offers Up to 173,000 Tenge State Subsidy for Housing Deposits

Kazakhstanis have until the end of December to top up their housing deposits with Otbasy Bank and receive a state subsidy of up to 173,000 tenge. The subsidy amounts to 20% of housing savings, capped at 200 monthly calculated indicators (MRPs). The maximum state payout is 173,000 tenge per depositor.
