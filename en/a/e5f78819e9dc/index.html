Iranian Official Confirms US Strike on Qeshm Island in Strait of Hormuz

An Iranian official has confirmed that the United States struck Qeshm Island in the strategic Strait of Hormuz on the night of November 8. Hormozgan provincial deputy governor Ahmad Nafisi stated that "critical infrastructure on the island was not damaged." Iranian media reported the strike occurred away from populated and commercial areas, with the situation under control. US Central Command has not commented on the strikes.
