Kyrgyzstan Proposes Memorandum of Understanding for Parliamentary Cooperation with Saudi Arabia

Kyrgyzstan's Speaker of Parliament, Marlen Mamataliev, has proposed a memorandum of understanding for cooperation between the parliaments of Kyrgyzstan and Saudi Arabia. The proposal was discussed during a meeting with Abdullah bin Mohammed bin Ibrahim Al-Sheikh, Chairman of Saudi Arabia's Consultative Council. Mamataliev expressed interest in expanding bilateral ties, including inter-parliamentary engagement, trade, and investment.
