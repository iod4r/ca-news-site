Kyrgyzstan Aims for Over 8% Annual Economic Growth by 2030

Kyrgyzstan plans to achieve an average annual economic growth rate exceeding 8% and increase investment in fixed capital to 20% of GDP by 2030, according to its National Development Program. The government intends to allocate significant funds to social services, business support, and infrastructure projects, with specific budget allocations outlined for education, healthcare, and social protection.
