Kazakhstan President Tokayev Orders Support for Rural Producers on Marketplaces

Kazakhstan's President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev has signed a decree to develop the digital economy, aiming to help rural producers sell goods through marketplaces and retail chains. Akimats will train rural entrepreneurs in online sales and provide consultations. The government is tasked with linking business support measures with marketplace, banking, and logistics services.
