Kazakh court rejects NCOC's challenge against environmental fine collection

A court in Kazakhstan's Atyrau region has denied North Caspian Operating Company N.V. (NCOC) its claim to declare the collection of an environmental fine illegal. The company sought to nullify a July 21 order related to the enforcement of a fine for exceeding sulfur storage limits at the Kashagan field.
