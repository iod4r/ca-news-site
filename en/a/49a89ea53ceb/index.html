Nearly 250 Gas Cylinders Seized in Illegal Circulation in Uzbekistan

Uzbek authorities have confiscated 249 domestic gas cylinders found to be illegally stored or misused within a single day. The seized liquefied gas, totaling 1.16 thousand kilograms, could have supplied 58 families for a month. Joint inspections by state security, interior ministry, prosecutor's office, and emergency services uncovered violations, including improper storage and unauthorized use by gas supply workers and private individuals.
