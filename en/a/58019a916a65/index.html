Afghan Activist Calls for 'Gender Apartheid' to Be Recognized as Crime Against Humanity

Human rights activist Tamanna Zaryab Paryani urged the international community at the UN Security Council to recognize "gender apartheid" as a crime against humanity. She highlighted the exclusion of Afghan women from public life and the lack of accountability for rights violations. Paryani stressed that women's rights must be central to the Council's activities and that targeting women is a threat to international peace and security.
