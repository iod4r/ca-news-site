China-Nepal Border Landslide: 16 Dead, 546 Missing; Climate Change Cited

A landslide on the China-Nepal border has resulted in 16 deaths and 546 missing persons. Experts attribute the disaster to glacier instability caused by long-term climate warming. Chinese authorities have allocated over $30 million for rescue efforts, with over 2,000 rescuers on site. 261 foreign nationals are among the missing.
