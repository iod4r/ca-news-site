Kazakhstan Plans to Cover 2026 Budget Deficit of 4.6 Trillion Tenge Through Borrowing

Kazakhstan's budget deficit for 2026 is projected at 4.6 trillion tenge, which the government plans to cover through domestic and international borrowing. The Ministry of Finance intends to raise $3.5 billion and 1.6 trillion tenge from external markets, including loans from the World Bank and ADB.
