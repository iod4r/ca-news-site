Ukraine has repatriated 14 children, aged 6-17, from Russian-occupied territories in the Kherson region. The regional administration reported that one 16-year-old boy was forced into military registration by Russian forces. Since the start of the year, 204 children have been returned from occupied Kherson, and over 2,600 since February 2022.
