Kazakhstan's Orphan Housing System Fails to Provide Homes After Decades of Waiting

Over 43,000 orphans in Kazakhstan are waiting for housing, with many facing denial even after 18 years. A recent case highlighted how income checks now prevent orphans from receiving allocated apartments. Changes in housing distribution, with 'Otbasar Bank' taking over from 2025, have further complicated the process, leaving applicants uninformed.
