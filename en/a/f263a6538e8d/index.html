Kyrgyzstan's National Bank to Mandate ISO 27001 Certification for Commercial Banks

The National Bank of Kyrgyzstan is transitioning from recommending to mandating ISO/IEC 27001:2022 information security standard compliance for commercial banks. The updated requirements, aligned with international standards since 2021, are in the final stages of preparation after public discussion. This move aims to enhance information security across the banking sector.
