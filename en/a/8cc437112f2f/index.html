Section of Abylai Khan Avenue in Almaty to be Closed This Weekend for LRT Construction

A section of Abylai Khan Avenue in Almaty, between Raimbek Avenue and Mametova Street, will be completely closed to traffic from October 10th to October 11th due to Light Rail Transit (LRT) construction. Warning signs will be installed, and drivers are advised to plan their routes in advance. Bus and trolleybus routes will also be rerouted.
