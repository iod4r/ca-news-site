Taliban Restrict Afghan Women's Access to Medical Care

In most Afghan provinces, male doctors are now prohibited from examining or treating women unless a male guardian is present, even in emergencies. Medical workers report Taliban inspections and threats of imprisonment. The shortage of female medical staff is exacerbated by a ban on women's medical education and employment.
