Kyrgyzstan Aims for 20% Annual Growth in Foreign Investment by 2030

Kyrgyzstan plans to achieve an 8% average annual real GDP growth and a 20% annual increase in foreign direct investment by the end of 2030. President Sadyr Japarov announced this, tasking the government with developing export-oriented models and modernizing trade policies. Efforts will also focus on protecting private property and simplifying investor procedures.
