Kazakhstan Telecom Investment Exceeds $2.2 Billion, Rural Connectivity Lags

Kazakhstan's telecom sector investment surpassed $2.2 billion in three years, yet only 2,606 of 6,179 villages have fiber-optic backbone access. The government aims for over 90% of villages to have high-speed internet by end of 2027. A World Bank project supports local network construction in 1,123 villages for 445,000 households.
