Uzbekistan to Host First-Ever BRICS+ Science Week Across All Regions

Uzbekistan will host the BRICS+ Science Week from November 2nd to 8th, marking the first time the event will span all regions of the country. The V International Festival 'Science 0+' in Tashkent on November 7-8 will be a central event. The festival, part of Russia's Decade of Science and Technology, aims to promote scientific literacy and engagement.
