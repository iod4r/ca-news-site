Kyrgyzstan Seeks Participation in Global Childhood Cancer Platform

Kyrgyzstan's Health Minister met with representatives from St. Jude Children's Research Hospital in the US during an international summit in Samarkand. Discussions focused on Kyrgyzstan's potential involvement in the Global Platform for Childhood Cancer, aimed at improving diagnosis and treatment of childhood cancers worldwide. Both sides affirmed commitment to enhancing pediatric oncology care in Kyrgyzstan.
