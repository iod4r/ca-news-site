Jeti-Oguz District Industrial Output Reaches 128.5 Billion Som

Industrial production in Kyrgyzstan's Jeti-Oguz district grew from 71.1 billion som in 2021 to 128.5 billion som in 2025, including output from the Kumtor mine. The district's total investment is projected at 21.4 billion som. Projects include drinking water initiatives and 81 bridges, with plans for a ski base and tourism development.
