Mongolia's President Resigns Voluntarily for the First Time in History

Ukhnaagiin Khurelsukh has become the first president in Mongolia's history to resign voluntarily. The parliament accepted his resignation with 92.3% of votes. Parliamentary powers have been transferred to the Speaker of Parliament, Sandagiin Byambatsogt, who will serve until a new president is elected.
