Kyrgyzstan Proposes Eliminating VAT on Potato Exports to Uzbekistan

During a visit to Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan proposed eliminating VAT on potato exports. This initiative aims to expand agricultural cooperation between the two countries, following President Sadyr Japarov's directive. Both nations agreed to ensure uninterrupted potato supplies and establish a joint venture for agricultural product processing.
