Kazakhstan Allocates 35.8 Billion Tenge for Maslikhat Deputy Elections

Kazakhstan plans to allocate 35.8 billion tenge for the upcoming maslikhat deputy elections scheduled for November 29. The Central Election Commission (CEC) confirmed the budget, which is comparable in scale to previous Kurultai elections. A significant portion will cover election commission member compensation.
