Tokayev Appoints Commander for Kazakhstan's Special Operations Forces

President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev has appointed Sayat Isanov as the commander of the Special Operations Forces of the Armed Forces of Kazakhstan. Isanov, a lieutenant colonel, previously served as deputy commander. His responsibilities include overseeing combat, tactical, and airborne training for elite units. Details of his career were not publicly disclosed due to the nature of special forces service.
