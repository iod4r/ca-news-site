EAEU sets Kyrgyzstan's 2027 meat import tariff quotas to ensure food security

The Eurasian Economic Commission has established tariff quotas for Kyrgyzstan's meat imports for 2027, totaling 65,000 tonnes. Quotas include 58,000 tonnes for poultry, 5,000 for beef, and 2,000 for pork. This measure aims to prevent shortages, balance supply and demand, and protect local producers.
