Kazakhstan's Education Infrastructure Transformed Over Seven Years

Over the past seven years, Kazakhstan has significantly developed its education infrastructure, increasing student capacity and reducing triple-shift and dilapidated schools. Since 2019, 1,342 new schools have been built, creating 1.1 million student places. The national project 'Future Schools' has added 217 new schools.
