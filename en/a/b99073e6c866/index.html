Aibek Dadebay Elected Speaker of Kazakhstan's New Parliament

Aibek Dadebay has been elected Speaker of Kazakhstan's new Parliament. Deputies of the Kurultai unanimously supported his candidacy in a secret ballot. President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev had previously nominated Dadebay, who was the leader of the Adilet party and former presidential chief of staff.
