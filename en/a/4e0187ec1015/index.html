Almaty Authorities Identify Vehicle Transport as Primary Air Pollution Source

Almaty city administration has identified vehicle transport as the main contributor to air pollution, accounting for approximately 60% of annual emissions. Private homes using coal and wood, as well as businesses like bathhouses and eateries, also contribute, especially in colder months. Natural factors like temperature inversions exacerbate the problem.
