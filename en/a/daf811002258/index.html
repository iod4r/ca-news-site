Kyrgyzstan's 'Ala-Too Resort' to auction 11 remaining lots after opening

Following the completion of its first phase, Kyrgyzstan's 'Ala-Too Resort' ski complex will auction 11 remaining investment lots. Of the initial 50 lots, 39 have been sold to domestic investors. The resort, declared a project of state significance, will feature approximately 250 kilometers of ski slopes across three zones and aims to attract up to 4 million tourists annually.
