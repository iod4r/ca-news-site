Kazakhstan and France Develop Master Plan to Preserve Lake Balkhash Until 2040

Kazakhstan is collaborating with French experts to create a Master Plan for the preservation of Lake Balkhash by 2040. Initial field research has been completed. Water inflow from the Ili River remains at an average long-term level, helping to stabilize the lake's water level. The Ministry of Water Resources confirmed these findings.
