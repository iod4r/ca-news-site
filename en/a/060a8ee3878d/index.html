Two oil tankers explode near Strait of Hormuz, Iran reports

Two oil tankers exploded near the Strait of Hormuz after hitting mines, according to Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps. One tanker exploded near the entrance to Oman's Hasab port, and the other in the eastern part of the strait. Iran stated the ships' crews had turned off identification systems and attempted to pass through the waterway on unapproved routes.
