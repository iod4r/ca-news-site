Caspian States Convene in Ashgabat to Advance Marine Scientific Research Cooperation

Turkmenistan hosted a meeting of Caspian Sea littoral states in Ashgabat to discuss cooperation on marine scientific research. Delegates reviewed a draft agreement aimed at facilitating research in the Caspian Sea, where countries aim to develop oil and gas reserves while addressing environmental concerns. The initiative was first proposed in 2018.
