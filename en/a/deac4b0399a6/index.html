Two Former Commanders Arrested in Connection with Kyrgyz Military Helicopter Crash

Two former commanders of the Kyrgyz Air Force, including ex-commander Kylychbek Aidaraliev and a unit commander Daniyar Davitahunov, have been arrested. They face charges related to violating flight safety regulations, leading to a fatal Mi-8MTV helicopter crash in January 2024. Both have been detained until December 2026.
