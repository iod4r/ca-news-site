Kyrgyz farmers sell fallen apples for low prices due to processing shortage

Farmers in Batken region, Kyrgyzstan, are selling fallen apples for 3 soms per kilogram due to a lack of processing facilities. Some apples fall from trees due to disease, affecting about 10 percent of orchards. Local residents resell these apples to Uzbekistan, where processing is more developed. Farmers have advocated for processing plants and cold storage for 20 years.
