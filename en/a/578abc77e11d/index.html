Pakistan confirms airstrikes on 10 locations in Afghanistan following drone incursions

Pakistan confirmed conducting precision aerial and drone strikes on 10 locations in Afghanistan, targeting sites used for launching drones. The strikes followed alleged "deliberate and unlawful incursions" by drones from Afghan territory. Local sources reported strikes in Khost, Kandahar, and Paktia provinces, with no casualties reported by the Taliban governor's spokesman in Khost.
