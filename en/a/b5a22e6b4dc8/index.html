Kazakhstan Plans Two-Stage Conscription and Drone Force Creation

Kazakhstan is planning a military reform including a two-stage conscription model and the creation of drone forces, aiming for implementation by 2027. The reform also involves revising the army's structure and enhancing combat readiness. Additional measures against hazing and improvements in military education and resource utilization are also part of the plan.
