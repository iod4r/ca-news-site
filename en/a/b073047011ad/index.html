Kazakhstan to Build Over 21 New Logistics Centers by 2030

Kazakhstan plans to construct more than 21 new logistics centers by 2030, adding to its existing 46 facilities. This expansion aims to enhance the country's terminal infrastructure and ensure the loading of transit cargo along corridors. The development also includes establishing an external terminal network in partner countries.
