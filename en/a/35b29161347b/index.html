Kazakhstan President Tokayev Vows No Division in Anti-Corruption Fight

President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev stated there will be no division between 'insiders' and 'outsiders' in the fight against corruption. He highlighted that over 3,168 corruption offenses were detected in the last three years, with a significant portion involving public servants. Tokayev emphasized that all corruption allegations will be thoroughly investigated by the Anti-Corruption Service.
