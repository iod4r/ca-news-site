Uzbekistan Considers Tax Breaks for Educational Material Publishers

A proposed law in Uzbekistan aims to grant tax exemptions on property and land for publishers of educational materials. To qualify, at least 90% of a company's revenue must come from producing these materials. The bill has passed its third reading in the Legislative Chamber and moves to the Senate for further review.
