EU May Extend Sanctions to Kyrgyzstan's Entire Crypto Sector

The European Commission has proposed extending sanctions to Kyrgyzstan's cryptocurrency sector as part of the 22nd package against Russia. If approved, European companies would be prohibited from engaging in direct or indirect operations with crypto-asset service providers registered in Kyrgyzstan. This move aims to prevent circumvention of existing sanctions.
