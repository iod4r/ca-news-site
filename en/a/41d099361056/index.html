Former Head of Uzbekistan Migration Agency Appointed to New Role at 'Vatandoshlar' Foundation

Behzod Musaev, formerly leading Uzbekistan's Migration Agency, has been appointed chairman of the 'Vatandoshlar' public foundation. The foundation, established in 2021, fosters connections with and supports Uzbek citizens abroad. Musaev's dismissal from the agency in September followed corruption scandals, including allegations of involvement in illegal schemes for labor migration to South Korea.
