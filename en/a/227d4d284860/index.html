Pakistani Forces Kill Two Drivers on Chaman-Spin Boldak Route

Sources in Afghanistan's Spin Boldak district report that Pakistani military personnel shot and killed two Afghan drivers. The drivers were attempting to enter Afghanistan illegally with the consent of Pakistani forces and the Taliban. Smuggling routes are reportedly being used for travel between the two countries following the Taliban's closure of official crossing points.
