Kazakhstan to Build Five New Modern Children's Hospitals

Kazakhstan plans to construct five modern, multidisciplinary children's hospitals with a total capacity of 1000 beds. New medical facilities will be located in Atyrau, Aktobe, Almaty, Shymkent, and Kaskelen, improving access to specialized care closer to home. Discussions were held with the Asian Development Bank and the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank.
