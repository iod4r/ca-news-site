Kazakhstan Border Guards Seize 59 Attempts to Illegally Export Currency in Ten Days

Kazakhstan's Border Service has thwarted 59 attempts to illegally transport undeclared currency totaling over 508 million tenge in a ten-day period. The seizures occurred between November 16 and 25 at border crossing points. The KNB reminded citizens that exporting cash foreign currency exceeding $10,000 is prohibited.
