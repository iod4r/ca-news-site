Kyrgyzstan's Industrial Enterprises Spent 13 Billion Som on Innovations in 2025

Kyrgyzstan's industrial enterprises invested 13.05 billion som in innovations in 2025, with only 4.3% of surveyed companies engaged in innovative activities. This marks a slight increase from the previous year. The National Statistical Committee reported that 39 enterprises were involved, with most processes nearing completion. 25 new products were launched.
