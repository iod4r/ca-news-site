Armenia Loses Voting Rights in CSTO Due to Unfulfilled Obligations

Member states of the Collective Security Treaty Organization (CSTO) have decided to suspend Armenia's voting rights within the organization. Armenia will also be unable to appoint representatives to responsible positions within the CSTO. This decision stems from Armenia's failure to fulfill its obligations for several years, according to Dmitry Peskov, the press secretary of the Russian president.
