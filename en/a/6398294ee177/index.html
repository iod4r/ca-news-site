Kazpost and Kazakhtelecom Obligated to Pay Monthly Compensation to Disabled Woman

Kazpost and Kazakhtelecom have been ordered by a court to pay monthly compensation to a woman in the Zhambyl region who became a first-group disabled person after a building roof collapsed on her. The companies must pay 177,000 tenge monthly until she reaches retirement age.
