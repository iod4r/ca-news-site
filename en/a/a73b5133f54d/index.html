Uzbekistan Inflation: Consumer Estimates Significantly Exceed Official Figures

A survey by Uzbekistan's Central Bank reveals that consumers estimate inflation at 9.2% for September, considerably higher than the official rate of 6.4%. The survey of 2,726 residents showed a nationwide estimate decrease from August's 10.1%. Tashkent recorded the highest regional estimate at 11.9%, while pensioners estimated 12.5%.
