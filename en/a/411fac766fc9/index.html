Relatives of Convicted Doctor Appeal to Kyrgyzstan President for Pardon

Relatives of nephrologist Nasira Beishebaeva, sentenced to three years in prison, have appealed to Kyrgyzstan's President Sadyr Japarov for her pardon. They expressed their hope for state intervention during a meeting with the president. Japarov promised to review the appeal. Beishebaeva's case involves complications during a patient's treatment, raising concerns among medical professionals about setting a precedent.
