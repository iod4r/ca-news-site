Kazakhstan Prosecutor General's Office Transfers Fraud Case to Regional Police

Kazakhstan's Prosecutor General's Office has transferred materials related to an alleged fraud case at the 'Manka' deposit to the East Kazakhstan regional police department. The investigation, initiated on June 15, 2026, is being conducted under Article 190, Part 4, Clause 2 of the Criminal Code, concerning fraud in particularly large amounts. The prosecution is monitoring the case.
