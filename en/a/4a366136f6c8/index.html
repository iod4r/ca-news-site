Kyrgyzstan Appoints New Ambassador to Pakistan, Recalls Envoy from Europe

President Sadyr Japarov has appointed Aidit Erkin as the new Ambassador of Kyrgyzstan to Pakistan. Erkin was concurrently recalled from his posts as Ambassador to Belgium, the Netherlands, and Luxembourg, and as Permanent Representative to the European Union. He had held the Brussels-based positions since November 2022.
