Li Auto to begin vehicle assembly in Kazakhstan this autumn

Li Auto will commence vehicle production in Kazakhstan this autumn, starting with large-node assembly and potentially moving to small-node assembly. Kazakhstan is the first country outside China to produce the brand's hybrid models L6, L7, and L9 at the SaryarkaAvtoProm plant in Kostanay. The plant aims to produce up to 10,000 vehicles annually.
