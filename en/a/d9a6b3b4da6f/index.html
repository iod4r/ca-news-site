New 'Halka' Substation and Transmission Lines Launched in Tashkent Region, Costing $171.4 Million

A new 500 kV 'Halka' substation and 285 km of high-voltage transmission lines have become operational in Uzbekistan's Tashkent region. The $171.4 million project, supported by the World Bank, aims to enhance power supply reliability for Tashkent and surrounding areas, and reduce load on the Tashkent Thermal Power Plant.
