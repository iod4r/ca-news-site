Kyrgyzstan Improves Irrigation Systems, Enhancing Agricultural Land

Kyrgyzstan has repaired and cleaned over 656 kilometers of inter-farm canals, improving the condition of 5,860 hectares of irrigated land. The Ministry of Water Resources, Agriculture, and Processing Industry reported that 638 hydraulic structures and 317 water metering posts were also repaired. This initiative aims to increase the efficiency of irrigation water delivery and improve agricultural land.
