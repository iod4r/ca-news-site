US Sanctions Tajik-Russian Citizen for Aiding Iran's Arms Procurement

The United States has added Alisher Domulodjanov, a citizen of Tajikistan and Russia, to its sanctions list. The Treasury Department's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) stated that Domulodjanov facilitated the procurement of man-portable air-defense systems for Iran. The sanctions also target Belarusian company Armory Alliance and Nigerian firm Vanguard Tactical Supply Limited.
