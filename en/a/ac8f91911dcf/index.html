Court Denies Bail for Activist Sanjar Bokayev, Citing Potential Influence

A court in Almaty has denied a request to change the pre-trial detention measure for civil activist Sanjar Bokayev and his videographer Ulan Nusipkozha, as well as activist Madiyar Kalmenov. The judge cited insufficient grounds, stating they could 'influence participants in the process.' Nusipkozha's measure was changed to house arrest due to health reasons.
