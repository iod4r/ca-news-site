Amnesty International Launches Global Campaign Against Gender Apartheid in Afghanistan

Amnesty International has launched a global campaign, '16 Days of Activism Against Gender-Based Violence,' to highlight gender apartheid in Afghanistan to international decision-makers. The campaign, running from November 25 to December 10, aims to raise public awareness and urge action, as Afghanistan has fallen off the media and political radar due to recent global crises. Amnesty calls for support to ensure Afghan women and girls are not forgotten.
