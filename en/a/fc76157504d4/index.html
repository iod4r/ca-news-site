Kazakhstan and Belgium Sign 22 Agreements on Education and Science

Following a forum of rectors, Kazakhstan and Belgium signed 22 agreements and memorandums. These agreements aim to foster academic exchanges, scientific research, and joint educational programs. The Kazakh Ambassador to Brussels met with Kazakh students in Belgium and Luxembourg to discuss new initiatives and opportunities arising from the state visit of King Philippe and Queen Mathilde to Kazakhstan.
