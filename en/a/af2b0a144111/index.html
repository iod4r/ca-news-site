Tajikistan Presents Preparations for UN Water Conference in Rome

Tajikistan's First Deputy Foreign Minister presented the country's preparations for the upcoming UN Water Conference in Dushanbe (May 4-6, 2028) at the 44th UN-Water meeting in Rome. The conference is the concluding event of the International Decade for Action 'Water for Sustainable Development' (2018-2028). Tajikistan emphasized its commitment to global water agenda continuity and cooperation.
