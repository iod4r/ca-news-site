Kazakh MP proposes biometrics for money transfers over 50,000 tenge to combat crime

A Kazakh Member of Parliament has proposed implementing biometric verification for money transfers exceeding 50,000 tenge. The measure aims to protect youth from involvement in drug crimes and 'droppering' (acting as money mules), citing statistics on drug-related offenses and youth involvement.
