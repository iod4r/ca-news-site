Kyrgyz President Warns of Politically Motivated Ethnic Unrest Ahead of Election

Kyrgyz President Sadyr Japarov warned that politicians are attempting to provoke violence between ethnic Kyrgyz and Uzbeks to disrupt the upcoming presidential election. Japarov's warning comes amid increased restrictions on opposition and independent media, raising concerns about political opponents being framed as national security threats.
