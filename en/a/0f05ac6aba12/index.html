Iran's Non-Oil Exports to Afghanistan Increase by 32 Percent

Iran reports a 32% increase in the weight of non-oil exports to Afghanistan during the first seven months of the current solar year compared to the same period last year. The total weight exported was 745,700 tons, valued at $247.59 million, a 10% rise in value. Imports from Afghanistan also saw a slight increase.
