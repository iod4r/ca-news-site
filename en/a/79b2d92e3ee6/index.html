Majority of Kazakh Families Live on Monthly Incomes Between 150,000 and 300,000 Tenge

A National Bank survey revealed that 30.9% of Kazakh families earn between 150,000 and 300,000 tenge monthly, making it the most common income bracket. Another 23.6% reported incomes between 300,000-500,000 tenge, while 13.4% earn over 500,000 tenge. A significant portion, 12.6%, earns 100,000-150,000 tenge.
