Kyrgyz MP Demands Explanation for $25 Million Fuel Subsidy Spending

A Member of Parliament in Kyrgyzstan has demanded an explanation for the use of over $25 million allocated for subsidized fuel for farmers. The funds were channeled through a state bank to a private company. The MP questioned the high cost of fuel despite the subsidy and requested details on how the money was spent. The head of the State Material Reserves stated that subsidies were not received and fuel is sold at market prices, with state reserves offering lower prices.
