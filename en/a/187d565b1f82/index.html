Afghanistan's Economic Growth Fails to Improve Household Incomes

Afghanistan's economy is projected to grow by 3% in 2026, but GDP per capita is expected to decline by 3.3% due to population growth. Despite economic expansion, declining purchasing power, limited jobs, and difficulty meeting basic needs remain challenges. Experts emphasize that economic growth should also be measured by quality of life improvements.
