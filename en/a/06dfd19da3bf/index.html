CAREC Tourism Working Group Discusses Sustainable and Transboundary Tourism Development

The CAREC Tourism Working Group met in Tashkent to boost regional tourism cooperation and set priorities for 2026-2027. Supported by Uzbekistan's Tourism Committee and the ADB, the meeting focused on reviving the working group's activities and implementing a tourism development MoU. Priorities include digitalization, capacity building, and modernizing the 'Visit Silk Road' portal.
