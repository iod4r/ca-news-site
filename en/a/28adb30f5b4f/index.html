Kazakhstan's Financial Regulator to Introduce Additional Prudential Measures for Banks

Kazakhstan's Agency for Regulation and Development of the Financial Market (ARFR) is developing additional prudential measures for banks to support long-term financing of investment projects. ARFR Chair Madina Abylkassymova stated that banks must actively participate in investment lending, becoming key partners for economic development. Measures include using future regulated income as collateral for infrastructure modernization loans.
