AMANAT Party Withdraws from Kazakhstan's Maslikhat Elections

The AMANAT party has voluntarily withdrawn from the upcoming elections for the Maslikhats (local representative bodies) in Kazakhstan. The Central Election Commission has approved seven other political parties to participate. AMANAT had previously merged with the "Adilet" party and had also opted out of earlier Kurultai elections.
