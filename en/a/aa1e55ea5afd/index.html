Kazakhstan to Launch Waste-to-Energy Plants by 2028 with Chinese Investment

Kazakhstan will introduce waste-to-energy facilities, with three plants to be built in Astana, Almaty, and Shymkent by 2028 through a $650 million investment from Chinese companies. These facilities aim to reduce landfill waste and generate electricity and heat. Similar projects are being considered for other regions.
