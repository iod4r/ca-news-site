Kazakhstan Reports 18.4% Decrease in Theft Incidents This Year

The number of theft crimes in Kazakhstan has decreased by 18.4% in the first 10 months of this year. Apartment burglaries dropped by 23.3%, and livestock theft by 6.7%. Over 9,000 individuals were apprehended, and the activities of 1,248 criminal groups were disrupted.
