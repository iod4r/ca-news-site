Uzbekistan and Afghanistan Agree to Boost Bilateral Trade and Exports

Uzbekistan and Afghanistan aim to expand trade and regional export links, with bilateral turnover reaching $1.38 billion in the first nine months of 2026. Uzbekistan's exports to Afghanistan increased by 34% year-on-year to $1.19 billion, while imports from Afghanistan rose by 47% to $192.7 million. Discussions focused on improving market access and addressing trade obstacles.
