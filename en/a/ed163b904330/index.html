Cars Account for 60% of Almaty's Emissions, Other Pollutants Identified

Cars are responsible for approximately 60% of Almaty's emissions, totaling about 113,000 tons annually. The city's ecology department stated that private homes heating with coal and wood, as well as businesses using solid and liquid fuel, also contribute significantly to air pollution, especially in colder months. Natural factors like temperature inversion also play a role.
