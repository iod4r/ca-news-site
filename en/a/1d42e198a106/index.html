Kazakh Employers Illegally Earned Nearly 5 Million Tenge by Falsifying Elderly Employment Records

Employers in Kazakhstan's East Kazakhstan region have been found to have illegally earned approximately 4.6 million tenge by falsely reporting full working days for elderly individuals employed under the 'Silver Age' state project. The prosecutor's office discovered that time sheets reflected actual work despite employees' absence, leading to unjustified budget subsidies. The funds have since been returned to the state.
