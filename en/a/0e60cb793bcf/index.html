S&P Raises Uzbekistan's Credit Rating to BB+ with Stable Outlook

International rating agency S&P Global Ratings has upgraded Uzbekistan's long-term sovereign credit ratings in national and foreign currency to BB+ with a stable outlook. This marks the highest rating in the country's history, bringing it one step closer to investment grade. The upgrade reflects systemic economic reforms and prudent financial policies.
