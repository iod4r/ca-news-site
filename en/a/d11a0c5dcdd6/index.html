Uzbekistan Appoints New First Deputy Minister of Energy

Uzbekistan's Ministry of Energy has seen a change in leadership. Umid Mamadaminov has moved to head the Agency for the Development and Regulation of the Energy Market. Abdumalik Nazirov, formerly head of "National Electric Networks of Uzbekistan," has been appointed as the new first deputy minister. Both appointments were made by presidential decree.
