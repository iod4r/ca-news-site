Kyrgyzstan ready to host CSTO joint military exercises in 2027

President Sadyr Japarov announced Kyrgyzstan's readiness to host Collective Security Treaty Organization (CSTO) joint military exercises in 2027. Speaking at a CSTO session in Moscow, Japarov highlighted Kyrgyzstan's commitment to collective security and practical cooperation within the alliance. He emphasized the importance of such events for enhancing combat readiness and coordination among collective forces.
