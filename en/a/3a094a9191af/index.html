Kazakhstan launches 'Umai' mortgage program for women with 15% down payment

Kazakh women can apply for the 'Umai' housing program starting August 31. The maximum loan is 50 million tenge, with a down payment of 15-20%. The program offers an annual interest rate of 14.8%. The Asian Development Bank has provided nine billion tenge for the first phase. Eligible women must have at least six months of official income and a family's average monthly net income not exceeding 1.25 million tenge.
