Kazakhstan Sees Sharp Decline in Young Entrepreneurs Since Year Start

The number of young entrepreneurs (under 35) in Kazakhstan has decreased by 18.4%, or 128,700, since the beginning of the year, as of October 1. The decline is observed in both urban and rural areas. While women constitute a larger portion of young business owners, their numbers also decreased. Trade and vehicle repair remain the most popular sectors.
