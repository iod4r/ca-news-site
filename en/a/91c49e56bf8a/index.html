Activists in Germany Urge Court Not to Forget Dilmurod Ergashev's Fate

A group of activists and human rights defenders in Germany held a protest urging the Düsseldorf court not to be indifferent to the fate of Tajik activist Dilmurod Ergashev. Ergashev was deported from Germany to Tajikistan in November 2024 and sentenced to eight years in prison for allegedly inciting extremism. The court is reviewing the legality of his deportation.
