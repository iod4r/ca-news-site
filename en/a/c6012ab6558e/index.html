Over 744 Vehicles with Summer Tires Turned Back Due to Bad Weather in Kazakhstan

In Kazakhstan's Akmola region, over 744 vehicles using summer tires were turned back from republican roads due to adverse weather conditions. Police temporarily restricted movement on several routes to ensure driver safety. These measures aim to prevent road accidents, as drivers were repeatedly warned to switch to winter tires.
