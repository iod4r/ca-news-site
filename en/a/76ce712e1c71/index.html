New Law on Constitutional Court Approved by Senate, Mandating Law Harmonization

A new law concerning the Constitutional Court has been approved by the Senate. It mandates that any body which adopted a normative legal act found to be unconstitutional must harmonize that act within three months. The law also clarifies the procedure for reviewing court decisions based on such unconstitutional acts.
