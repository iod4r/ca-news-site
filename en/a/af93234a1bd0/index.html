New Moscow Rules: Minimum Earnings for Kyrgyz Migrants from 2027

Moscow City Duma has approved new rules establishing a minimum income level for labor migrants starting in 2027. Migrants without families will need to earn around 60,000 rubles monthly, while families of three will need about 80,000 rubles. These new income requirements will also affect citizens of Kyrgyzstan, who are part of the Eurasian Economic Union.
