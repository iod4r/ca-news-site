UAE joins Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan in trans-Afghan railway project

Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan have signed a memorandum of understanding with the United Arab Emirates for the development of a trans-Afghan railway. This project aims to provide Central Asian exports access to Pakistani seaports. The UAE's involvement addresses the financial and technical shortcomings of the Central Asian states in realizing this infrastructure goal.
