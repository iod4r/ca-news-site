Private Education Organizations in Astana Protest Mandatory Face ID Attendance Tracking

Approximately 30 heads of private supplementary education organizations in Astana protested the mandatory use of Face ID for tracking children's attendance. This requirement is part of a pilot project for state-funded extracurricular activities launched in 2025. The authorities aim to ensure transparent and targeted use of budget funds by verifying actual student participation, preventing unjustified financial claims. Private organizations argue against this method.
