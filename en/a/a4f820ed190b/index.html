Tajikistan Waives Import Duties on Fuel from Non-Free Trade Zone Countries Until 2026

The government of Tajikistan has established a zero import customs duty rate for petroleum products and gases from countries not participating in the Free Trade Zone Agreement. This measure will be in effect until the end of 2026. The decision aims to reduce the cost of fuel imports from specific nations.
