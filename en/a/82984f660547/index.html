AIIB Signs $1.1 Million in Grants for Caspian Green Energy Projects

The Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB) has signed two grant agreements totaling $1.1 million with Azerbaijan to support green energy initiatives in the Caspian Sea. Funds will aid preparations for a submarine electricity cable between Kazakhstan and Azerbaijan and preliminary planning for offshore wind infrastructure. Uzbekistan is also expected to be connected.
