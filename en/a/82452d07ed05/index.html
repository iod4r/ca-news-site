Uzbekistan's Namangan Region Aims for $5.4 Billion in Foreign Investment by 2026

The Namangan region in Uzbekistan plans to attract $5.4 billion in foreign investment and achieve $1.2 billion in exports by the end of 2026. Further growth is projected for 2027, with targets of $7-8 billion in foreign investment and $1.3 billion in exports. New investment proposals totaling $5.4 billion have been prepared to meet these goals.
