Kazakhstan to Label Official Government Responses to Media Inquiries

Kazakhstan's Minister of Culture and Information, Arman Kyryqbayev, announced an initiative to label official government responses to media requests. This measure aims to help citizens distinguish between official information and misinformation amidst a flood of false narratives. The proposed norm has been supported by deputies and will be implemented through a by-law after the law is passed.
