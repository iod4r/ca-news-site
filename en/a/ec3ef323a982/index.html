Afghanistan and Turkmenistan Establish Joint Road Transport Commission

Afghanistan and Turkmenistan agreed to create a joint commission for road transport and transit facilitation following talks in Herat. The commission will address transport issues, improve coordination, and ease visa procedures for drivers and traders. The agreement aims to strengthen bilateral cooperation and Turkmenistan's role as a regional transport hub.
