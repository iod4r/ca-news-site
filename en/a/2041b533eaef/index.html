Turkmenistan Targets 80 Billion Cubic Meters of Gas Production in 2026, TAPI Deliveries by 2027

Turkmenistan aims to produce approximately 80 billion cubic meters of natural gas in 2026 and 2027, with plans to increase oil production to eight million metric tons in 2026. Gas deliveries via the Turkmenistan-Afghanistan-Pakistan-India (TAPI) pipeline to Afghanistan are targeted for mid-2027, pending Afghan readiness. The country currently supplies 40 billion cubic meters of gas to China and Uzbekistan.
