Kazakhstan Health Minister Comments on Attack on Doctor in Aktau Hospital

Kazakhstan's Health Minister Akmaral Alnazarova addressed an assault on a doctor at a children's hospital in Aktau. A man attacked the doctor after his child was denied hospitalization. The minister stated that an investigation is underway to assess the actions of both parties involved. She emphasized increased accountability for attacks on medical professionals, noting around 50 criminal cases have been initiated nationwide.
