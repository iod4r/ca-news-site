College Director Fired, 45 Students Expelled After Prosecutor's Inspection in Kostanay Region

Following a prosecutor's inspection at a college in the Kostanay region, the director has been fired and 45 students expelled. Seven teachers faced strict disciplinary action. The inspection revealed violations in educational processes, state property management, and student safety, including students not attending classes and improper handling of vehicle write-offs. Dormitory repairs are also planned.
