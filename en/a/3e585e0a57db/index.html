Kazakhstan MP Warns of Unlicensed Broker Advertisements in Media

A member of the "Ak Zhol" faction in Kazakhstan's parliament has raised concerns about Kazakh media outlets promoting unlicensed foreign investment projects. MP Yuri Li reported that over 160 publications about 48 projects were found on 51 online resources between 2023 and 2026, lacking required financial regulator licenses. He has requested a comprehensive investigation by the Prosecutor General's Office and the Ministry of Culture and Information.
