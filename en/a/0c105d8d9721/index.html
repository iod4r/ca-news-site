Nurbek Bukuev appointed Deputy Minister of Health of Kyrgyzstan

Nurbek Bukuev has been appointed Deputy Minister of Health of Kyrgyzstan, replacing Bakytbek Kadyraliev. Bukuev, a surgeon and oncologist-gynecologist with 21 years of experience, previously headed the National Center of Oncology and Hematology in Bishkek. He is also an associate professor and author of over 20 scientific works.
