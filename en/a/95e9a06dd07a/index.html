Uzbekistan to raise liquefied gas price for population by 2.5 times from November 15

From November 15, Uzbekistan will increase the retail price of liquefied gas for the population by 2.5 times, from 2,000 to 5,000 Uzbek soums per kilogram. The new price includes VAT. Compensations will be provided for low-income families.
