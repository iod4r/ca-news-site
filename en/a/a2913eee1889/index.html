Kazakhstan to Approve Digital Economy Development Plan by December 1

Kazakhstan's President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev has signed a decree on developing a 'people's digital economy.' The government must approve an implementation plan by December 1, consolidating state support measures for entrepreneurs. By year-end, authorities will develop a simplified legal regime for digital employment and expand special tax regimes.
