Kazakhstan: Company Director Sentenced to Six Years for Wheat Fraud and Debt

A director of 'Eclipse Agro' in Kazakhstan has been sentenced to six years in prison. He was found guilty of selling 352 tons of wheat belonging to a farming cooperative without consent and failing to pay transport services totaling 11.1 million tenge. The court also prohibited him from holding certain positions for six years.
