Taliban Finance Ministry Increases Import Tariffs on Six Goods

The Taliban's Ministry of Finance has announced an increase in customs duties for six imported goods to support domestic industry. Tariffs rose for bottles, paint, leather shoes, plastic shoes, gypsum, and baby diapers. The duty on heart springs was reduced. Afghanistan heavily relies on imports, with over $11 billion in annual imports.
