Kazakhstan Plans to Release at Least 10 Tigers into the Wild by 2033

Kazakhstan aims to restore the population of the extinct Turkestan tiger, planning to release at least 10 tigers into natural habitats by 2033. The long-term goal is to reach a population of 50 tigers. The program involves initial releases followed by efforts to ensure natural reproduction.
