FBI Accuses North Korea of Stealing Over $6 Billion from Binance

The FBI has accused North Korea of being behind a cyberattack on the Binance crypto exchange, resulting in the theft of over $6 billion. The exchange has pledged to cover financial losses and announced a phased resumption of withdrawals. The FBI linked the attack to North Korean cyber activity known as TraderTraitor.
