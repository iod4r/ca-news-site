Book on Islam Karimov's Main Opponent Presented in Istanbul and Samarkand

A two-volume biographical book, "The Man Who Woke Up Early," about Uzbek poet, dissident, and politician Muhammad Salih, the founder of the "Erk" opposition party, was presented in Istanbul. The book, authored by Karimberdi Turamurod, covers Salih's life in Uzbekistan until 1993 and his life in exile from then until the present. A presentation also took place in Samarkand.
