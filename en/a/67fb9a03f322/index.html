Russia Plans to Ban Migrants from Working in Taxis in Eight Regions

Russia intends to prohibit labor migrants from working in taxi services in eight regions starting in 2027. Another region may ban foreign nationals from courier delivery. These restrictions could affect citizens of Tajikistan and Uzbekistan, who require work permits for employment in Russia. The Ministry of Labor drafted the proposal.
