Kazakhstan-Linked Hackers Used AI to Steal Over 2,000 Tajik Defense Documents

Cybersecurity firm CloudSEK reports that hackers linked to Kazakhstan used an AI tool, Hermes Agent, to steal over 2,000 documents from Tajikistan's Defense Ministry. The stolen data includes flight requests and military personnel transfers. The group, Silent Lynx, gained access via a railway employee's account.
