Kyrgyz citizens can still enter Kazakhstan without prior authorization

Kazakhstan has introduced new digital migration mechanisms, including the QazETA pre-entry approval system. However, Kyrgyzstan's Foreign Ministry clarified that citizens of Kyrgyzstan do not currently need prior authorization to travel to Kazakhstan. A Kyrgyz MP met with the Kyrgyz Consul General in Almaty to discuss the new rules and the protection of Kyrgyz citizens' rights.
