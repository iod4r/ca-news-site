Turkish Official Questions Russia's Eligibility for Turkic States Observer Status

A senior Turkish official has questioned Russia's eligibility for observer status in the Organization of Turkic States (OTS), citing language requirements. The rules, adopted in 2021, mandate that observer countries must recognize a Turkic or related language as a state or official language for nationwide government correspondence. Russia's federal language is Russian, though some regions have Turkic languages.
