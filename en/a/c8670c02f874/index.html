Kazakhstan Allocates Up to 50 University Study Places Annually for Kyrgyzstan Citizens

Kazakhstan offers up to 50 university study places annually for citizens of Kyrgyzstan. Of these, 30 are for Kyrgyz citizens and up to 20 for ethnic Kazakhs with Kyrgyz citizenship. Quotas are allocated for IT, engineering, construction, agriculture, medicine, and pedagogy, with specific numbers for bachelor's and master's programs.
