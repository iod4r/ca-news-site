Migrants in Russia to Pay More for Children Under New Tax Law from 2027

Starting January 1, 2027, foreign workers in Russia with work permits will face increased monthly tax payments if they have minor children residing in Russia. An additional 50% will be added for each child. This applies to children under 18, including adopted ones. The tax amount varies by region.
