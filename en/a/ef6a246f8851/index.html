Kazakh National Security Committee Arrests Kazatomprom Top Manager in Samruk-Kazyna Corruption Case

Kazakhstan's National Security Committee (KNB) has arrested Kuanych Omargbekov, a board member of Kazatomprom, as part of a corruption investigation into Samruk-Kazyna. He and others were detained on November 23 and placed in a temporary detention facility, suspected of involvement in a corruption scheme organized by former Samruk-Kazyna leadership.
