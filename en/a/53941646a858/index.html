New special economic zone in Abay region to create 14,000 jobs

Kazakhstan has established a new special economic zone, 'Semey,' in the Abay region, aiming to attract 1.4 trillion tenge in investments. The 600-hectare zone will host high-tech enterprises, including those for export, and is expected to create approximately 14,000 permanent jobs. This initiative aligns with the president's goal to boost manufacturing.
