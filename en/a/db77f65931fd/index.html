Stigma and Misconceptions Prevent Afghans from Seeking Mental Health Treatment

Specialists in Afghanistan report that stigma, fear of being labeled "crazy," and misconceptions about psychiatric medication prevent many individuals from seeking or continuing mental health treatment. Families sometimes attribute problems to weak faith or witchcraft, opting for religious healers over doctors, which can worsen conditions.
