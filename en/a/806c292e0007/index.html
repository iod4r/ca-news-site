Parliamentary Committee Approves Almazbek Moldobaev for Constitutional Court Judge Position

The Jogorku Kenesh Committee on Judicial and Legal Issues, Law Enforcement, Crime Prevention, and Anti-Corruption has approved Almazbek Moldobaev's candidacy for the position of judge of the Constitutional Court. The committee's conclusion will be sent to the Jogorku Kenesh for further consideration. The committee also supported the dismissal of Keneshbek Toktomambetov from the Supreme Court.
