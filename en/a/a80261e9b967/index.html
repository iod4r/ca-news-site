Kazakhstan Issues Storm Warning for October 30: Freezing Temperatures, Snow, and Ice Expected

A storm warning has been issued for October 30 across 16 regions of Kazakhstan, with forecasts of rain, snow, ice, fog, and wind gusts up to 20 m/s. Freezing temperatures are expected in the south and southeast. Specific regions like Pavlodar, East Kazakhstan, Abai, and North Kazakhstan will experience mixed precipitation and icy conditions. Jetisu and Almaty regions anticipate night frosts, with temperatures dropping significantly in mountainous areas.
