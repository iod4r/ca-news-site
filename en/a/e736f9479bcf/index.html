Three Snow Leopards Filmed in Kyrgyzstan Amidst Expanding Conservation Efforts

Three snow leopards were filmed in Kyrgyzstan's Naryn region during a survey by the Ministry of Natural Resources. This sighting comes as Kyrgyzstan expands its conservation efforts, including the opening of the Ak Ilbirs research and rehabilitation center. Kyrgyz and Russian scientists will collaborate on genetic studies and animal rehabilitation.
