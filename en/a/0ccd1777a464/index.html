Uzbekistan: Qoshtepa Canal Won't Fully Utilize Amu Darya Water Until 2030, Says Minister

Uzbekistan's Water Resources Minister Shavkat Khamrayev stated that Afghanistan's Qoshtepa Canal will not begin full water withdrawal from the Amu Darya until 2030. Construction is ongoing, and Uzbekistan is in constant dialogue with Afghanistan on transboundary water resource use. While Afghanistan has a right to use Amu Darya water, the volume and terms require agreement.
