International Court of Justice holds Myanmar responsible for Rohingya genocide

The International Court of Justice has ruled that Myanmar is responsible for the genocide of the Rohingya people. This marks the first time a state has been held accountable for genocide by the court. The ruling concerns the actions of Myanmar's security forces in Rakhine State in 2017. The court ordered Myanmar to take measures to investigate crimes and provide reparations.
