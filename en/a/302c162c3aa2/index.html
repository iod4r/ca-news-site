Tajikistan Reports on Human Rights at UN, Receives Recommendations on Torture and Domestic Violence

Tajikistan presented its report on human rights at the 53rd session of the UN Human Rights Council, focusing on stricter penalties for torture, preventing domestic violence, and protecting women's and children's rights. The delegation, led by the Justice Minister, highlighted legal reforms, including increased prison sentences for torture and the inapplicability of the 2026 amnesty law to such offenses.
