Average Pension in Uzbekistan Increased 3.3 Times Over Ten Years

The average monthly pension in Uzbekistan reached 1,649,200 soums in 2025, a 3.3-fold increase from 494,200 soums in 2016, according to the National Statistics Committee. Recent years show steady growth, with pensions rising from 848,500 soums in 2020 to 1,495,600 soums in 2024. The total increase over a decade amounts to 1,155,000 soums.
