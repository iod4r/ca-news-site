Kazakhstan Launches Platform for Collective Debt Settlement

Kazakhstan has launched Finkelisim.kz, a platform developed by the Financial Market Development Agency and the First Credit Bureau. It allows citizens with multiple loans from various institutions to apply for collective debt settlement through mediation. The platform aims to help borrowers negotiate a unified payment schedule with creditors.
