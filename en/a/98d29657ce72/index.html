Civil Society Groups Raise Concerns Over ICC's Separate Afghanistan Investigation

Civil society organizations have expressed concern over the International Criminal Court's decision to create a separate investigative situation, dubbed 'Afghanistan Situation 2'. They fear this could complicate criminal proceedings, delay victims' access to justice, and impact ongoing investigations. The ICC's Pre-Trial Chamber ordered the separate investigation based on a referral from six countries.
