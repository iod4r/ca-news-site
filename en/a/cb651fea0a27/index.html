Bishkek Eco-Plant Processes Over 400,000 Tons of Waste in First Year

Bishkek's waste incineration plant processed 401,500 tons of waste and generated 187.9 million kWh of electricity in its first year of operation. The facility currently handles 1,000 to 1,200 tons of solid waste daily, with a power generation capacity of 30 MW. Authorities plan to triple the plant's capacity by constructing a second unit, which will increase total waste processing to 3,000 tons per day and electricity generation to 90 MW.
