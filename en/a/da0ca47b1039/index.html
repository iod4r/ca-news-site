Kazakhstan: Defense's Fifth Attempt to Remove Judge in Bokayev Case Denied

In Kazakhstan, defense lawyers for civil activist Sanzhar Bokayev were denied their fifth request to remove Judge Zhanat Abenova, citing suspicions of bias. The prosecution alleges Bokayev defrauded 42 million tenge in 2018 through a scheme involving a gas vehicle mileage event. The defense argues the court is not adhering to the scope of the investigation.
