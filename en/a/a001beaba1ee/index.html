Uzbekistan's Central Bank Explores Wholesale Digital Currency and Stablecoin Models

Uzbekistan's Central Bank is studying the feasibility of creating a wholesale Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) for interbank settlements. The bank is also exploring private stablecoin models. Officials stated that a white paper on CBDC is being prepared, and international experiences are being analyzed.
