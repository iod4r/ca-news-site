UNICEF Facilitates Humanitarian Aid to Afghanistan via Uzbekistan Hub

UNICEF is utilizing a hub in Termez, Uzbekistan, to deliver critical humanitarian aid, including therapeutic food and medicine, to Afghanistan. The organization highlighted the hub's crucial role in combating malnutrition, with millions of children at risk. Timely delivery of supplies is essential for meeting the needs of vulnerable children and families across Afghanistan.
