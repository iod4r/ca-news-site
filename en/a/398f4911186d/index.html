Georgia Deports 91 Foreign Nationals, Including Uzbek Citizens, Banning Re-entry

Georgia's Ministry of Internal Affairs has deported 91 foreign citizens, including individuals from Uzbekistan, and banned them from re-entering the country. This action is part of intensified measures against illegal migration, with stricter legislation simplifying deportation procedures. Citizens from Azerbaijan, Armenia, Egypt, India, Jordan, Iraq, China, Congo, Nigeria, Pakistan, Russia, Sudan, Tajikistan, Thailand, Turkmenistan, Turkey, and Uganda were also expelled.
