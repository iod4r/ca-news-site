Kinda Pass in Kyrgyzstan's Naryn Region Closed Until April 2027

The Kinda Pass in the Naryn region of Kyrgyzstan, on the At-Bashy — Kinda — Torugart road, has been temporarily closed until April 1, 2027. The closure affects the section between kilometers 65 and 73. This decision was made due to expected heavy snowfall and avalanche risks during winter to ensure public safety.
