Bishkek coal reserves exceed 21,000 tons, prices vary by district

Coal reserves in Bishkek have surpassed 21,000 tons, a nearly one-third increase since mid-November. Forty-three coal bases operate across the capital, offering fuel at prices ranging from 7,000 to 7,800 soms per ton. The city administration reported that specific coal brands like 'Kara-Keche' and 'Shabyrkyul' have different price points depending on the district.
