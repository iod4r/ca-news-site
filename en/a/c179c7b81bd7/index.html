Sanitary Doctors Deny Responsibility for Construction Near Anthrax Sites in Kosshy

Sanitary doctors in Kazakhstan are not responsible for deciding where housing is built near livestock burial sites and anthrax outbreaks, according to the Sanitary Epidemiological Control Committee. They stated that since 2018, their specialists have not participated in land commission meetings that allocate plots for housing. The Ministry of Agriculture maintains the registry of burial sites, with local authorities responsible for zoning and implementing veterinary measures.
