Kazakh National Bank Supports Initiative to Protect Debtors' Funds from Full Write-off

The National Bank of Kazakhstan has backed a parliamentary initiative to legally protect a subsistence minimum on debtors' bank accounts during debt collection. Current rules vary depending on the collector. While banks can only seize 50% of funds for direct loan recovery, court bailiffs may deplete accounts entirely, leaving debtors without essential funds.
