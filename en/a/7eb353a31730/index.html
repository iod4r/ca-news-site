Kyrgyzstan to build 15-megawatt floating solar power plant

Kyrgyzstan plans to construct a 15-megawatt floating solar power plant near HPP-5, as reported by the Ministry of Energy. The project, a collaboration with the Asian Development Bank (ADB), is currently selecting a consultant. This new plant will be 150 times more powerful than the existing pilot floating solar station.
