Uzbekistan Plans to Cap Tax Penalty Amount to Debt Sum

Uzbekistan is planning to limit the amount of penalties for late tax payments to the principal tax debt. This proposal is part of a bill aimed at supporting entrepreneurs, which was approved in its first reading by the Legislative Chamber of the Oliy Majlis on October 27. The change aims to reduce administrative restrictions on entrepreneurial activities.
