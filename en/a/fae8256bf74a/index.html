Taliban Foreign Minister to Attend ECO Summit in Tehran

Amir Khan Muttaqi, the Taliban's acting foreign minister, will attend the 18th Summit of the Economic Cooperation Organization (ECO) in Tehran on December 3-4. The summit will focus on regional economic cooperation, trade, and transit. Notably, the Taliban was not invited to the previous ECO summit in Islamabad.
