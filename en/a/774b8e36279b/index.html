New 220/110/10 kV Substation to Enhance Dushanbe's Power Supply

A new 220/110/10 kV electrical substation is planned near Dushanbe, aiming to improve the reliability of electricity supply to the capital and surrounding areas. The project, funded by the Tajik government and the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA), is part of the 2022–2030 energy sector development plan.
