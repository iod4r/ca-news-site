Court hears case of alleged theft of granite tiles during Almaty river reconstruction

A court in Almaty heard testimony regarding the alleged theft of granite tiles during the reconstruction of the Bolshaya Almatinka river. The prosecution claims Sanjar Bokayev and his videographer organized the theft and created a video blaming city officials. A representative from the ecology department testified to reputational damage.
