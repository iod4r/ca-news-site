Tajikistan's Ministry of Energy Inspects Dushanbe's CHP-2 Plant Readiness for Winter

Tajikistan's Deputy Minister of Energy and Water Resources, Sharif Mahmadzoda, visited Dushanbe's Heat and Power Plant (CHP-2) to assess its preparation for the winter period. The plant has an installed capacity of 400 MW of electricity and 235 Gcal of heat. Ensuring reliable operation is crucial for supplying the capital's residents and institutions.
