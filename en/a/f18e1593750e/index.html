Over 3,400 Vehicles Fail Environmental Checks in Almaty Pilot Program

In Almaty's pilot LEZ project, 3,435 out of 18,581 tested vehicles failed environmental checks between September 1 and November 24 due to exceeding emission standards. Carbon monoxide was the most common pollutant. Approximately 64% of vehicles passed re-inspection after repairs.
