International Congress on Pediatric Oncology and Hematology Held in Samarkand

The second international congress on pediatric oncology and hematology, themed "From Recovery to Improving Quality of Life," took place in Samarkand as part of the "Investing in Children's Well-being" Global Summit. Health ministers from Uzbekistan, Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, and Tajikistan, along with WHO and SIOP representatives, attended. The congress highlighted progress in Uzbekistan's national strategy and specialized centers.
